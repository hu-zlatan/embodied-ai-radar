- ⭐⭐⭐ **[MuJoCo](https://mujoco.org/)** (2012) — Fast, accurate contact physics; the default simulator for robot learning research, open-sourced by DeepMind. · [code](https://github.com/google-deepmind/mujoco)
- ⭐⭐ **[When the Simulator Fails, Not the Policy: Auditing Contact Artifacts in Simulated Robot Benchmarks](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.41857)** (2026) — Many benchmark 'policy failures' are physics-engine ejections. SimuGuard traced RoboTwin artifacts to one collision constant; changing it cut artifact episodes from 12/100 to none and lifted five tasks 89% → 98%. · [code](https://github.com/ALEXander22568/simuguard)
- ⭐ **[Genie Sim 3.0 : A High-Fidelity Comprehensive Simulation Platform for Humanoid Robot](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.02078)** (2026) — Genie Sim 3.0: a manipulation simulation platform whose LLM tool builds scenes from natural language, with an LLM- and VLM-based automated benchmark. · [code](https://github.com/AgibotTech/genie_sim)
- ⭐ **[Isaac Lab: A GPU-Accelerated Simulation Framework for Multi-Modal Robot Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.04831)** (2025) — The reference paper for Isaac Lab, Orbit's successor — GPU-parallel physics, rendering and sensor simulation with ready-made robots and tasks for RL and imitation. · [code](https://github.com/isaac-sim/IsaacLab)
- ⭐ **[MuJoCo Playground](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.08844)** (2025) — GPU-accelerated MJX environments for locomotion and manipulation that train in minutes and transfer to real robots. · [code](https://github.com/google-deepmind/mujoco_playground)
- ⭐ **[Newton](https://github.com/newton-physics/newton)** (2025) — Open, GPU-accelerated physics engine built on NVIDIA Warp and OpenUSD with multiple solvers for contact-rich robot learning; co-developed by Disney Research, Google DeepMind and NVIDIA, hosted by the Linux Foundation.
- ⭐ **[RoboTwin 2.0: A Scalable Data Generator and Benchmark with Strong Domain Randomization for Robust Bimanual Robotic Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.18088)** (2025) — Simulation pipeline that writes and checks task code with multimodal LLMs to mass-produce bimanual demos under heavy randomization (clutter, lighting, backgrounds, instructions); also a 50-task dual-arm benchmark. · [code](https://github.com/RoboTwin-Platform/RoboTwin)
- ⭐ **[GRUtopia: Dream General Robots in a City at Scale](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.10943)** (2024) — Simulated interactive 3D society for many robot types; its GRScenes set has 100k annotated scenes across 89 categories that combine into city-scale worlds. · [code](https://github.com/OpenRobotLab/GRUtopia)
- ⭐ **[Genesis](https://github.com/Genesis-Embodied-AI/Genesis)** (2024) — An open-source, Python-native physics platform covering rigid bodies, soft bodies, fluids and fast GPU simulation.
- ⭐ **[Humanoid-Gym: Reinforcement Learning for Humanoid Robot with Zero-Shot Sim2Real Transfer](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.05695)** (2024) — Isaac Gym–based RL framework for humanoid locomotion with a sim-to-sim check in MuJoCo before deploying zero-shot to real humanoids; a common starting point for humanoid RL. · [code](https://github.com/roboterax/humanoid-gym)
- ⭐ **[ManiSkill3: GPU Parallelized Robotics Simulation and Rendering for Generalizable Embodied AI](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.00425)** (2024) — GPU-parallel simulation and rendering with many manipulation tasks; fast visual RL and data generation. · [code](https://github.com/haosulab/ManiSkill)
- ⭐ **[RoboCasa: Large-Scale Simulation of Everyday Tasks for Generalist Robots](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.02523)** (2024) — 120 kitchen scenes, 2,500+ objects and 100 tasks with generated demonstrations for training generalist policies. · [code](https://github.com/robocasa/robocasa)
- ⭐ **[Habitat 3.0: A Co-Habitat for Humans, Avatars and Robots](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.13724)** (2023) — Adds simulated humanoid avatars and a human-in-the-loop interface to Habitat, so robots can be trained and evaluated on tasks shared with people, such as social navigation and social rearrangement. · [code](https://github.com/facebookresearch/habitat-lab)
- ⭐ **[ManiSkill2: A Unified Benchmark for Generalizable Manipulation Skills](https://arxiv.org/abs/2302.04659)** (2023) — Unified manipulation benchmark built to add object-level geometric and topological variation that earlier suites lacked. · [code](https://github.com/mani-skill/ManiSkill) · [project](https://maniskill2.github.io/)
- ⭐ **[Orbit: A Unified Simulation Framework for Interactive Robot Learning Environments](https://arxiv.org/abs/2301.04195)** (2023) — The framework that became Isaac Lab — modular robots, sensors and tasks on top of Isaac Sim. · [code](https://github.com/isaac-sim/IsaacLab)
- ⭐ **[BEHAVIOR-1K: A Human-Centered, Embodied AI Benchmark with 1,000 Everyday Activities and Realistic Simulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.09227)** (2022) — 1,000 household activities chosen from a survey of what people want robots to do, defined in a logic language and simulated in OmniGibson with realistic physics and rendering. · [code](https://github.com/StanfordVL/BEHAVIOR-1K)
- ⭐ **[Brax: A Differentiable Physics Engine for Large Scale Rigid Body Simulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2106.13281)** (2021) — JAX physics engine that runs thousands of environments in parallel on GPU/TPU together with the RL training loop, cutting locomotion training from hours to minutes. · [code](https://github.com/google/brax)
- ⭐ **[dm_control: Software and Tasks for Continuous Control](https://arxiv.org/abs/2006.12983)** (2020) — DeepMind's Python stack on top of MuJoCo — bindings, PyMJCF and Composer for building models and tasks, the standard Control Suite benchmarks, plus locomotion and arm-and-bricks manipulation tasks. · [code](https://github.com/google-deepmind/dm_control)
- ⭐ **[robosuite: A Modular Simulation Framework and Benchmark for Robot Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2009.12293)** (2020) — Modular MuJoCo-based manipulation environments behind many imitation-learning benchmarks. · [code](https://github.com/ARISE-Initiative/robosuite)
- ⭐ **[AI2-THOR: An Interactive 3D Environment for Visual AI](https://arxiv.org/abs/1712.05474)** (2017) — Photorealistic interactive indoor scenes where agents open, pick up and change objects; the base of many embodied-AI benchmarks such as ALFRED. · [code](https://github.com/allenai/ai2thor)
- ⭐ **[Gazebo](https://gazebosim.org/)** (2004) — The long-standing open-source robot simulator of the ROS ecosystem — sensors, plugins and ROS 2 integration; the usual choice for testing full robot software stacks rather than training policies. · [code](https://github.com/gazebosim/gz-sim)
- **[AM-Bench: A Modular Simulation Suite and Benchmark for Aerial Manipulation Policy Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.00641)** (2026) — Benchmark for multirotor aerial manipulation: under-, fully and over-actuated platforms, 12 tasks, configurable wind and saturation, standard low-level controllers and learning baselines for system-level comparison.
- **[Active Generative Simulation: Meta-Learning What to Simulate for Sim-and-Real Policy Co-Training](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.19479)** (2026) — MetaGenSim learns which simulated data to generate, using meta-gradients from real-world performance, instead of sampling the simulator uniformly. · [project](https://meta-gensim.github.io)
- **[Alloy: A Multi-Material Physics Engine for Robot Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.16312)** (2026) — Open-source engine on Newton, MuJoCo Warp and Warp: rigid robots, cloth, cables, liquids, granular and soft bodies coupled in one scene, many worlds per GPU. Built for coding agents (MCP tools). · [code](https://github.com/argonrobotics-ai/alloy)
- **[AuRA: From Atoms to Bits through Autonomous Robot Interaction](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.34452)** (2026) — Automates the robot interaction and data preparation behind physics-informed digital twins; reports 88.5% less active human labor per asset across 13 deformable objects. · [code](https://github.com/Yoonkyo/AuRA) · [project](https://aura-robot.github.io/)
- **[Closing the Dynamics Gap through Self-Directed Robotic Interaction](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.02691)** (2026) — CUE (CoRL 2026 Late Breaking Results): an agent identifies friction and restitution by choosing interactions where its simulation is least accurate; on billiards it reports 72% lower held-out position error. · [project](https://cue-dynamics.github.io/)
- **[CoDA: Co-Evolution of Data Engines and Action Policies](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.22362)** (2026) — Turns diagnosed policy failures into new simulated scenes and shaped training signals, so generator and policy improve together; on LIBERO-Pro, reports success rising from 25% to 84% under position perturbations. · [project](https://plan-lab.github.io/projects/coda/)
- **[ComSim: Building Scalable Real-World Robot Data Generation via Compositional Simulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.11386)** (2026) — Compositional simulation: classical sim produces accurate action-video pairs and a neural simulator restyles them as real-looking video, in a real-sim-real loop seeded by a little real data. · [project](https://faceong.github.io/ComSim/)
- **[DexSuite: An All-in-One Framework for Dexterous Manipulation Across Arms, Hands, and Tasks](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.37799)** (2026) — Open dexterous-manipulation framework on Genesis: 30 tasks, 10 arms × 12 end effectors (100+ single-arm robots), teleoperation, simulated touch, a 16-pair benchmark and 4,700 demo episodes. · [project](https://dexsuiteorg.github.io)
- **[Do Rigid-Body Simulators Dream of Soft Robots? Learning Contact-Rich Manipulation for Tendon-Driven Continuum Robots](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.22397)** (2026) — Puts tendon-driven continuum robots natively into MuJoCo with a continuum-mechanics-based discretization, validated against Cosserat rods and hardware; sim-trained imitation policies transfer zero-shot to a real robot. · [code](https://github.com/ContinuumRoboticsLab/opencr-mujoco) · [project](https://continuumroboticslab.github.io/opencr-mujoco/)
- **[Effective Parameters, Real Behavior: Renormalization for Robotics -- From Infinite Electron Mass to Sim-to-Real Gap](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.24079)** (2026) — Renormalization view of sim-to-real: tune effective, sim-rate-dependent parameters instead of measured ones. - **[Elastra: A Simulation Framework for Elastic Bodies](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.15112)** (2026) — Simulates springy surfaces such as mattresses and trampolines with vertically moving elastic elements; residual RL on top of HoST lifts G1 stand-up success on mattresses from 44.1% to 72.5% (trampoline 55% → 89%).
- **[GIF: Agentic Generation of Interactive and Functional Object Compositions for Robot Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.05927)** (2026) — Agentic generation of functional object compositions (e.g. parts that must fit): disentangled mesh reconstruction, relative pose refinement under geometric and physical guidance, and a VLM verifier.
- **[Genie Sim Benchmark: Real-to-Sim Fidelity and Evaluation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.15020)** (2026) — Checks whether Genie Sim scores track the real world: on 22 paired real/sim robot-task pairs, π0.5 scores correlate at 0.97 (MAE 0.04), backed by joint-level position and torque identification studies.
- **[Gym-Anything for Robotics: Scaling physical simulations with digital agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.40565)** (2026) — Coding agents build robotics simulations, checked by an adversarial create-and-audit loop; result RoboWorld: 25 sims chosen by U.S. GDP share, 200 RL envs. Best frontier models average just 0.06 reward on test tasks. · [code](https://github.com/gym-anything/robotics) · [project](https://gym-anything.github.io/robotics)
- **[HyperDrone: Simulation-to-Reality Transfer at Millions of Frames per Second](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.28950)** (2026) — GPU batch-rendering quadrotor simulator; reports 20×+ the speed of existing batch quadrotor sims, with a new ray tracer rendering RGB, depth, segmentation, normals and optical flow (CoRL 2026).
- **[Kamino: GPU-based Simulation of Rigid Multi-Body Physics with Kinematic Loops](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.16536)** (2026) — CoRL 2026 Spotlight. - **[Learning Physics-Guided Residual Dynamics for Deformable Object Simulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.13451)** (2026) — PGRD simulates deformables with an optimizable spring-mass backbone plus learned residuals, reporting better accuracy than either alone; used for MPC and action-conditioned video prediction. · [code](https://github.com/shivanshpatel35/pgrd) · [project](https://pgrd-robot.github.io/)
- **[Lodos: A Lightweight Simulator Designed for Contact-Rich Interactions with Detailed Meshes](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.08872)** (2026) — GPU rigid-body simulator on NVIDIA Warp for detailed mesh–mesh contact; reports physics on par with established simulators at about 10x their throughput there. Lodos-v2 (2611.08868) adds exact frictional contact. · [code](https://github.com/martius-lab/lodos)
- **[MARE: An Open-Source Lunar Rover Simulator with Shadow Ground Truth for Perception and Autonomy](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.28465)** (2026) — Unreal Engine 5 + ROS 2 lunar rover simulator: generated cratered terrain, two rovers with stereo, lidar, event and SPAD cameras, pixel-accurate shadow ground truth for low-sun polar lighting, and a GNC stack. · [code](https://github.com/raulcastar/MARE-Lunar-Simulator)
- **[MolmoB0T: Large-Scale Simulation Enables Zero-Shot Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.16861)** (2026) — CoRL 2026 Oral. - **[SILO: Simulation-in-the-Loop Sim-to-Real Transfer for Multi-Stage Cable Routing](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.04616)** (2026) — Sim-to-real RL for multi-stage cable routing: GPU-parallel cable simulation, localized policies and a simulation-in-the-loop executor. Reports higher success and 2x shorter cycle times than prior learning methods. · [project](https://silo-cable-routing.github.io/)
- **[SIMPLE: Simulation-Based Policy Learning and Evaluation for Humanoid Loco-manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.08278)** (2026) — Humanoid loco-manipulation testbed pairing MuJoCo physics with Isaac Sim rendering: 16 whole-body tasks, 50 scenes, 1,000+ objects, motion-planning and VR data pipelines; benchmarks imitation, VLA and WAM policies.
- **[Scaling Sim-to-Real VLA Reinforcement Learning with Generative 3D Worlds](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.18532)** (2026) — Uses 3D world generative models to build 100 diverse interactive scenes for RL fine-tuning of a VLA. Reports sim success from 9.7% to 79.8% and real-world success from 21.7% to 75%. · [project](https://horizonrobotics.github.io/gail/scale-vla-rl)
- **[SceneBloom: Agentic Indoor Environment Generation with Realistic and Diverse Layouts for Embodied Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.32978)** (2026) — Generates sim-ready rooms by sampling furniture layouts with diffusion priors, then compiling them into collision-free 3D scenes; nav policies trained there beat ones trained on real scans on held-out real scans. · [code](https://github.com/Tinaliu0123/scenebloom)
- **[Sim-to-Real Transfer for Muscle-Actuated Robots via Generalized Actuator Networks](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.09487)** (2026) — Learns a neural actuator model from joint trajectories alone, combined with rigid-body sim, to transfer RL policies to a pneumatic-muscle arm. Claims the first sim-to-real transfer for a 4-DoF muscle-actuated arm. · [code](https://github.com/intelligent-soft-robots/genan) · [project](https://webdav.tuebingen.mpg.de/genan/)

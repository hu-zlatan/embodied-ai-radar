- ⭐ **[Do As I Can, Not As I Say: Grounding Language in Robotic Affordances](https://arxiv.org/abs/2204.01691)** (2022) — SayCan — an LLM proposes steps and learned value functions score what the robot can actually do right now.
- **[Aero-VPA: Few-Step Flow-Matching Control over Non-Reconstructive JEPA Latents for Agile Flight on Edge Hardware](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.04392)** (2026) — A compact JEPA-plus-flow-matching quadrotor policy with an unusually candid report: cloning failed all 60 missions, DAgger plateaued, and a runtime shield raised simulated forest success from 21.1% to 52.2%. · [code](https://github.com/ylee-sling/Aero-VPA) · [project](https://huggingface.co/ylee-sling/aero-vpa)
- **[Benchmarking Obstacle-Conditioned Safety in Household Robot Navigation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.34674)** (2026) — OSMA-Bench: matched scenes that differ only in obstacle type test whether navigation policies slow down and keep clearance by how risky an obstacle is. VLM and VLA policies recognise the risk but rarely act on it. · [code](https://github.com/OSMA-Bench/OSMA-Bench) · [project](https://osma-bench.github.io/)
- **[CoFL-S: Spatially Queryable Sector Flow Fields for Local Language-Conditioned Navigation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.02222)** (2026) — Low-level VLN action head that predicts a language-conditioned flow field over the visible sector and rolls it out into trajectories. Adds a continuous-time Habitat benchmark. · [code](https://github.com/ut-dragon-lab/CoFL)
- **[Collaborative Planning for UAV-UGV Teams under Operational Constraints via Masked Error-bounded Neuro-Symbolic Diffusion](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.18850)** (2026) — MEND plans UAV-UGV recharge teamwork with masked diffusion, enforcing operational rules at execution through a logic-based action mask; tested on real maps and physical robot teams.
- **[CostNav: A Navigation Benchmark for Real-World Economic-Cost Evaluation of Physical AI Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.20216)** (2026) — Scores delivery-robot navigation by money: cost, revenue and break-even, using Isaac Sim plus public filings and injury data. Reports that none of seven baselines is economically viable yet. · [code](https://github.com/worv-ai/CostNav)
- **[Cracking Mobile Manipulation: From Failure Diagnosis to Agentic Robot System](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.19600)** (2026) — Finds a VLA at 80% on atomic skills drops to 19% when composed into mobile manipulation, diagnoses why, and builds an agentic system around it. · [code](https://github.com/PKU-VaLuE-Lab/Cracking-Mobile-Manipulation)
- **[Driving, Fast or Slow? Neuro-Symbolic Guidance for Motion Prediction in Multi-Modal Ground Mobility](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.15251)** (2026) — TraCS adds interpretable, probabilistic traffic-rule logic to black-box motion predictors, generated from natural-language regulations; reports consistent gains on Argoverse 2. Oral.
- **[EmbodiedWorld-200K: Toward Embodied World Agents via Embodied-Planning Dataset and Interactive World Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.25959)** (2026) — 200K+ navigation-planning samples annotated from camera trajectories up to instructions; RL post-training inside a video world model, used as a cheap stand-in environment, reportedly helps on unseen scenes. · [code](https://github.com/XiaokunFeng/EmbodiedWorld-200K) · [project](https://xiaokunfeng.github.io/EmbodiedWorld-200K/)
- **[From Region Arrival to Instance-Level Grounding in Vision-and-Language Navigation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.03792)** (2026) — Argues VLN success metrics hide a 'last-3-meter grounding gap': agents stop nearby without facing the target. Adds instance-level metrics and REALM, a refinement module for any backbone.
- **[G2-Nav: Grounded and Guarded Vision-Language Costmaps for Robot Social Navigation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.16956)** (2026) — Social navigation where the VLM writes a costmap instead of choosing actions, checks upstream tracking, and a fast safety check guards against latency. Real-world tests. · [code](https://github.com/centiLinda/G2-Nav)
- **[Gaussian Episodic Memory for Long-Term Spatiotemporal Reasoning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.33158)** (2026) — GEMS keeps a 3D Gaussian Splatting map that is updated only where the scene changes, and an agent renders views on demand to answer when/where questions. Introduces GEMS-Bench (15 scenes, 473 QA pairs). · [project](https://abwerby.github.io/GEMS/)
- **[HAVANA: A Hierarchical Video-Action Model for Vision-Language Navigation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.24122)** (2026) — Dual-system video-action model for VLN: a slow system turns history and instruction into a compact latent plan, a fast one turns plan + latest view into local trajectories; no video is generated at inference. · [code](https://github.com/Anastasiawd/HAVANA) · [project](https://github.com/Anastasiawd/HAVANA)
- **[Hierarchical Residual Policy Learning for Real-World Mobile Manipulation with Sparse Human Guidance](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.20607)** (2026) — HiRe-MoMa fine-tunes a frozen mobile-manipulation policy in the real world by learning residual corrections, with only occasional human guidance. · [code](https://github.com/tasl-lab/HiRe-MoMa) · [project](https://hire-moma.github.io/)
- **[InJePA: Intention-First Latent Planning with Coupled JEPA World Models for Visual Navigation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.23896)** (2026) — Two coupled JEPAs share a latent space: one proposes goal-conditioned latent intentions, the other predicts consequences, closing an intention-action-consequence loop. Evaluated on image-goal navigation in Habitat MP3D. · [code](https://github.com/zhuxun132/InJepa)
- **[MOON: Learning When to Forget and How to Move On in Vision-and-Language Navigation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.33546)** (2026) — Off-route VLN agents keep following their stale history ('behavioral inertia'). MOON adds a deviation judge that clears it and a GRPO-trained recovery policy (<1% params); reports +5.2 / +6.0 points on R2R-CE / RxR-CE. · [code](https://github.com/AutoLab-SAI-SJTU/MOON)
- **[OSCAR: Obstacle Survival Curves for Adaptive Robot Navigation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.00990)** (2026) — CoRL 2026 Spotlight. Decides whether to wait or reroute at a blocked route by modeling how long each obstacle class tends to persist.
- **[OdorSpaces: Visual-Olfactory Embodied Navigation in 3D Environments](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.05738)** (2026) — Adds simulated odour transport to Habitat-Sim using Fire Dynamics Simulator, giving a smell-and-vision navigation benchmark for finding invisible gas leaks and early-stage fires, with a multimodal RL baseline.
- **[One Scan is Enough: Demonstration-Free Adaptation for Language-Guided Navigation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.33794)** (2026) — Adapts a language-guided navigation policy to a new building from one iPhone scan, compiled into a training environment with synthetic instructed episodes. Reports 85% real success vs 49% for a demo-fine-tuned baseline.
- **[OpenFlyV2: An onboard vision-language-action model for aerial perception and navigation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.39287)** (2026) — A single onboard VLA for drones: one shared vision-language representation serves semantic understanding, spatial reasoning and closed-loop waypoint control. · [code](https://github.com/Ideal-ljl/AeroBrain) · [project](https://ideal-ljl.github.io/AeroBrain/)
- **[RoboMemory: A Brain-inspired Multi-memory Agentic Framework for Interactive Environmental Learning in Physical Embodied Systems](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.01415)** (2026) — Brain-inspired memory for embodied agents (temporal, spatial, semantic, episodic) with a spatial knowledge graph. Reports +26.5 points for Qwen2.5-VL-72B on EmbodiedBench. · [code](https://github.com/SP4595/RoboMemory-Code)
- **[ScaleNav: Scalable Internet Video Pretraining for Language-Conditioned Local Navigation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.25690)** (2026) — Pretrains on internet egocentric video to learn how the view changes when approaching a described target, then aligns to actions in sim; reports 94.9% success on its HabitatGS-LN benchmark plus a real quadruped. · [code](https://github.com/MachEmbodied/ScaleNav) · [project](https://zju3dv.github.io/ScaleNav/)
- **[See What I Need, Dream What I Want: A Navigation Foundation Model on a Self-Constructed Representation Space](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31762)** (2026) — RepresentNav fuses several vision foundation models into a policy-learned space and predicts the next latent before acting (JEPA-style); reports 83.8% on VLNVerse fine-grained (+20.05 pts), real-robot tests. · [project](https://wang-yinxi.github.io/representnav/)
- **[SidewalkBench: Benchmarking Visual Navigation on Urban Sidewalks](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.16953)** (2026) — CoRL 2026 Spotlight. Benchmark for visual navigation on urban sidewalks, built on Isaac Sim with procedurally generated and real-world environments. · [code](https://github.com/VAIL-UCLA/SidewalkBench) · [project](https://vail-ucla.github.io/SidewalkBench/)
- **[TARSphere: A Robot-Oriented Full-Stack Framework for Long-Horizon Embodied Agents in Evolving Physical and Social Environments](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30043)** (2026) — Simulator with embodied NPCs, dialogue and event-driven task states, plus a skill harness for frozen MLLMs; its 360-episode bench reports general MLLMs can beat embodied ones; task updates and HRI stay hard. · [code](https://github.com/xuyicheng-zju/TARSphere) · [project](https://xuyicheng-zju.github.io/TARSphere/)
- **[WayTrace: A Map-to-Language-to-Trace Hierarchy for Embodied Urban Navigation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.05129)** (2026) — A vision-language model turns web-map directions into one atomic command at a time, a predictor draws the path in the image, and RL tracks it; a wheeled-legged robot completes real missions of up to 1.37 km. · [project](https://way-trace.github.io)
- **[NavDP: Learning Sim-to-Real Navigation Diffusion Policy with Privileged Information Guidance](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.08712)** (2025) — NavDP trains a navigation diffusion policy in simulation with privileged-information guidance, for sim-to-real use in dynamic open-world scenes. · [code](https://github.com/InternRobotics/NavDP) · [project](https://wzcai99.github.io/navigation-diffusion-policy.github.io)
- **[CANVAS: Commonsense-Aware Navigation System for Intuitive Human-Robot Interaction](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.01273)** (2024) — CANVAS lets people guide navigation with abstract cues such as verbal commands or rough sketches, even when they are noisy. · [project](https://worv-ai.github.io/canvas)
- **[Language-Grounded Dynamic Scene Graphs for Interactive Object Search with Mobile Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.08605)** (2024) — MoMa-LLM grounds LLMs in dynamic scene graphs for long-horizon object search with a mobile manipulator in unexplored spaces. · [code](https://github.com/robot-learning-freiburg/MoMa-LLM) · [project](https://moma-llm.cs.uni-freiburg.de)
- **[NaVILA: Legged Robot Vision-Language-Action Model for Navigation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.04453)** (2024) — A VLA outputs mid-level navigation commands in language that an RL locomotion policy executes, so legged robots follow natural-language routes; adds Isaac Lab VLN benchmarks. · [code](https://github.com/AnjieCheng/NaVILA) · [project](https://navila-bot.github.io/)
- **[NaVid: Video-based VLM Plans the Next Step for Vision-and-Language Navigation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.15852)** (2024) — A video VLM that reads the monocular RGB history plus the instruction and outputs the next navigation action directly, without maps, odometry or depth. · [code](https://github.com/jzhzhang/NaVid-VLN-CE)
- **[OK-Robot: What Really Matters in Integrating Open-Knowledge Models for Robotics](https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.12202)** (2024) — Combines off-the-shelf open-vocabulary models into a pick-and-drop system that works in real homes without training.
- **[Uni-NaVid: A Video-based Vision-Language-Action Model for Unifying Embodied Navigation Tasks](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.06224)** (2024) — Extends NaVid into one video VLA model for several navigation tasks (instruction following, object search, question answering, human following). · [code](https://github.com/jzhzhang/NaVid-VLN-CE)
- **[ETPNav: Evolving Topological Planning for Vision-Language Navigation in Continuous Environments](https://arxiv.org/abs/2304.03047)** (2023) — For VLN in continuous spaces, builds a topological map online from predicted waypoints, plans on it with a cross-modal transformer and moves with an obstacle-avoiding controller; reports >10%/>20% gains on R2R-CE/RxR-CE. · [code](https://github.com/MarSaKi/ETPNav)
- **[NavGPT: Explicit Reasoning in Vision-and-Language Navigation with Large Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.16986)** (2023) — A purely LLM-based VLN agent — GPT models read text descriptions of what the robot sees, its history and possible directions, then reason step by step about where to go; zero-shot on R2R, still behind trained models. · [code](https://github.com/GengzeZhou/NavGPT)
- **[NoMaD: Goal Masked Diffusion Policies for Navigation and Exploration](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.07896)** (2023) — One diffusion policy handles both goal-directed navigation and exploration by masking the goal.
- **[PIRLNav: Pretraining with Imitation and RL Finetuning for ObjectNav](https://arxiv.org/abs/2301.07302)** (2023) — PIRLNav — behavior cloning on human ObjectNav demonstrations, then RL fine-tuning of the same policy; the paper reports 65.0% success on Habitat ObjectNav, 5 points above the previous best. · [code](https://github.com/Ram81/pirlnav)
- **[VLFM: Vision-Language Frontier Maps for Zero-Shot Semantic Navigation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.03275)** (2023) — Zero-shot object-goal navigation that scores exploration frontiers by vision-language similarity to the target, with no task-specific training; tested in Habitat and on a Spot robot. · [code](https://github.com/rai-opensource/vlfm)
- **[ViNT: A Foundation Model for Visual Navigation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.14846)** (2023) — A Transformer navigation policy trained on many robots' driving data that adapts to new goal types and robots with light fine-tuning. · [code](https://github.com/robodhruv/visualnav-transformer)
- **[CoWs on Pasture: Baselines and Benchmarks for Language-Driven Zero-Shot Object Navigation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2203.10421)** (2022) — CLIP on Wheels pairs open-vocabulary localization (CLIP-style models, no fine-tuning) with classical exploration for zero-shot object navigation; the Pasture benchmark adds rare, attribute-described and hidden objects. · [code](https://github.com/real-stanford/cow) · [project](https://cow.cs.columbia.edu/)
- **[Code as Policies: Language Model Programs for Embodied Control](https://arxiv.org/abs/2209.07753)** (2022) — LLMs write Python that calls perception and control APIs, turning instructions into reactive robot programs.
- **[GNM: A General Navigation Model to Drive Any Robot](https://arxiv.org/abs/2210.03370)** (2022) — The predecessor of ViNT — one goal-conditioned navigation policy trained on pooled data from several different robots, which transfers to new robots including ones not seen in training. · [code](https://github.com/robodhruv/visualnav-transformer)
- **[Inner Monologue: Embodied Reasoning through Planning with Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2207.05608)** (2022) — Feeding success detection and scene descriptions back to the LLM planner lets it replan after failures.
- **[LM-Nav: Robotic Navigation with Large Pre-Trained Models of Language, Vision, and Action](https://arxiv.org/abs/2207.04429)** (2022) — Chains an LLM (parses landmarks from the instruction), CLIP (finds them in images) and a visual navigation model to follow route instructions on a real robot without language-annotated robot data. · [code](https://github.com/blazejosinski/lm_nav)
- **[Visual Language Maps for Robot Navigation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2210.05714)** (2022) — VLMaps fuses visual-language features into a 3D map so robots can navigate to open-vocabulary spatial goals.
- **[Beyond the Nav-Graph: Vision-and-Language Navigation in Continuous Environments](https://arxiv.org/abs/2004.02857)** (2020) — VLN-CE moves Room-to-Room instructions from a fixed navigation graph into continuous Habitat scenes with low-level actions; the standard setting for realistic VLN. · [code](https://github.com/jacobkrantz/VLN-CE)
- **[Learning to Explore using Active Neural SLAM](https://arxiv.org/abs/2004.05155)** (2020) — Splits exploration into a learned neural SLAM module, a global policy that chooses long-term goals and a local policy that reaches them, which is far more sample-efficient than end-to-end RL. · [code](https://github.com/devendrachaplot/Neural-SLAM)
- **[Object Goal Navigation using Goal-Oriented Semantic Exploration](https://arxiv.org/abs/2007.00643)** (2020) — SemExp builds an episodic semantic map and learns a policy that picks long-term goals on it, a modular recipe that became a standard ObjectNav baseline in Habitat. · [code](https://github.com/devendrachaplot/Object-Goal-Navigation)
- **[The Marathon 2: A Navigation System](https://arxiv.org/abs/2003.00368)** (2020) — The paper behind Nav2, the standard ROS 2 navigation stack - behavior-tree orchestration of planners, controllers and recovery behaviors, validated by long marathon runs on real robots. · [code](https://github.com/ros-navigation/navigation2)
- **[Vision-and-Language Navigation: Interpreting visually-grounded navigation instructions in real environments](https://arxiv.org/abs/1711.07280)** (2017) — Introduced the VLN task and the Room-to-Room (R2R) benchmark in Matterport3D homes.

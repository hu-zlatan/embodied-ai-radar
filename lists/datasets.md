- ⭐⭐⭐ **[Open X-Embodiment: Robotic Learning Datasets and RT-X Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.08864)** (2023) — 1M+ trajectories from 22 robot types pooled by 30+ labs; RT-X models trained on it beat single-robot baselines. · [code](https://github.com/google-deepmind/open_x_embodiment)
- ⭐⭐ **[LeRobot](https://github.com/huggingface/lerobot)** (2024) — PyTorch library with datasets, pretrained policies (ACT, Diffusion, SmolVLA) and drivers for low-cost arms.
- ⭐⭐ **[LIBERO: Benchmarking Knowledge Transfer for Lifelong Robot Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.03310)** (2023) — 130 language-conditioned tasks in four suites; now the most common simulation benchmark for VLA papers. · [code](https://github.com/Lifelong-Robot-Learning/LIBERO)
- ⭐ **[BEAKER: An Expert-Curated Benchmark for Embodied Brains in Self-Driving Chemical Laboratories](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.06911)** (2026) — 1,000 expert-curated questions over 11 image and video task types test multimodal LLMs as the brain of a self-driving chemistry lab: affordances, trajectories, apparatus setup, safety and step reasoning.
- ⭐ **[What Are We Actually Benchmarking in Robot Manipulation?](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.04233)** (2026) — CoRL 2026 Oral. Audits LIBERO, CALVIN, SimplerEnv, RoboCasa and RoboTwin 2.0 against four failure modes, from shortcut solvability to creeping overfitting. LIBERO and CALVIN fail several diagnostics. · [code](https://github.com/ripl/ManipulationBenchmarkAudit) · [project](https://ripl.github.io/manipulation_benchmark_audit/)
- ⭐ **[WireCraft: A Simulation Benchmark for Industrial DLO Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.18097)** (2026) — CoRL 2026 Spotlight. Simulation benchmark for wires and cables in industrial assembly, aimed at the gap left by benchmarks tied to specific hardware or lacking modular, customizable task assets.
- ⭐ **[RoboVerse: Towards a Unified Platform, Dataset and Benchmark for Scalable and Generalizable Robot Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.18904)** (2025) — MetaSim puts many simulators (Isaac Sim/Gym, MuJoCo, Genesis, SAPIEN…) behind one interface; adds a large synthetic dataset migrated from public benchmarks and unified IL/RL benchmarks. · [code](https://github.com/RoboVerseOrg/RoboVerse) · [project](https://roboverseorg.github.io/)
- ⭐ **[Evaluating Real-World Robot Manipulation Policies in Simulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.05941)** (2024) — SimplerEnv — real-to-sim environments whose scores correlate with real-robot results for RT-1/Octo-style policies. · [code](https://github.com/simpler-env/SimplerEnv)
- ⭐ **[CALVIN: A Benchmark for Language-Conditioned Policy Learning for Long-Horizon Robot Manipulation Tasks](https://arxiv.org/abs/2112.03227)** (2021) — Chains of five language instructions in a row test long-horizon, language-conditioned control. · [code](https://github.com/mees/calvin)
- ⭐ **[Habitat: A Platform for Embodied AI Research](https://arxiv.org/abs/1904.01201)** (2019) — A very fast photorealistic 3D simulator and task suite that standardized embodied navigation research. · [code](https://github.com/facebookresearch/habitat-sim)
- ⭐ **[Meta-World: A Benchmark and Evaluation for Multi-Task and Meta Reinforcement Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/1910.10897)** (2019) — 50 Sawyer manipulation tasks for multi-task and meta-RL evaluation. · [code](https://github.com/Farama-Foundation/Metaworld)
- ⭐ **[RLBench: The Robot Learning Benchmark & Learning Environment](https://arxiv.org/abs/1909.12271)** (2019) — 100 hand-designed manipulation tasks with demos; a staple for 3D policy methods like PerAct. · [code](https://github.com/stepjam/RLBench)
- ⭐ **[ScanNet: Richly-annotated 3D Reconstructions of Indoor Scenes](https://arxiv.org/abs/1702.04405)** (2017) — RGB-D scans of 1,500+ indoor scenes with 3D reconstructions, camera poses and semantic labels; the standard benchmark for 3D scene understanding that embodied perception builds on. · [code](https://github.com/ScanNet/ScanNet)
- **[ATHENA: Accelerated Multi-Task Heterogeneous Influence Functions for Robot Data Curation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.16208)** (2026) — Influence functions at billion-parameter VLA scale (about 313x faster via Kronecker structure and a rank-r Hessian approximation) for multi-task data curation; matches full-data fine-tuning with 50% of demos in sim.
- **[ATOM-Bench: A Real-World Benchmark for Atomic Skills and Compositional Generalization in Manipulation Policies](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.16826)** (2026) — CoRL 2026 Spotlight. Real-world benchmark that factorizes tabletop manipulation into motor and instruction atoms to test atomic skills and how policies recombine them. · [project](https://flageval-baai.github.io/AtomBenchPage)
- **[AstroBench: Benchmarking Free-Floating Capture and Space Robotic Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31064)** (2026) — Benchmark for catching drifting, tumbling objects inside a spacecraft with a fixed-base arm, plus storage and servicing tasks. Reports a frozen generalist policy captured only 2/200 static targets. · [code](https://github.com/AstroUpLab/AstroBench) · [project](https://astroup.com/astrobench/)
- **[AutoDex: An Automated Real-World System for Dexterous Grasping Data Collection](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.23689)** (2026) — AutoDex runs real dexterous-grasp trials, labels outcomes and resets objects with little human help: 3,500+ trials on 100 objects (Allegro, Inspire); about 0.2 human-hours per 500 trajectories vs 9.3 by teleop.
- **[BURGER: Benchmarking and Understanding Robotic Generalization via Embodied Drawing](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.40532)** (2026) — A language-conditioned drawing benchmark with open-ended valid solutions: 28,200 simulated demonstrations over 282 tasks, used to compare five VLA models on data scale, language grounding and multimodal behavior. · [code](https://github.com/Qxjyqzw/BURGER) · [project](https://siyingzhao.github.io/BURGER/)
- **[Behavior Spatial Benchmark: Evaluating Spatial Understanding for Robotics](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.34051)** (2026) — BEHAVIOR-Spatial, built on BEHAVIOR-1K: spatial QA, point grounding and robot action tests. A baseline policy drops from 41.6% on held-out layouts to 16.1% when the requested target changes; oracle target cues help most.
- **[Benchmarking Real-World Robotic Manipulation through Stochastic Challenges](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.12804)** (2026) — 'Stochastic challenges': reproducible real scenes with tasks sampled from a shared distribution, so robots in different labs compare by success rate like a hidden test set. Shown with two robots in two labs.
- **[Beyond Infraction Scores: Failure-Boundary Profiles for Closed-Loop Evaluation of Driving and Robot Navigation Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.06453)** (2026) — Sweeps one measured factor per scenario and certifies whether an agent should proceed or stop; finds the top learned CARLA agent at 0.60 decision accuracy against 0.74 for the rule-based expert. · [code](https://github.com/speedym/failure-boundary-profiles)
- **[BiDex-100: A Comprehensive Benchmark for Bimanual Dexterous Manipulation Across 100+ Simulation Tasks](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.25459)** (2026) — 106 sim tasks for two dexterous hands along five axes (generalization, instructions, few demos, long horizon, memory), each with an out-of-domain twin; 8,080 teleoperated demos; seven VLAs benchmarked. · [project](https://BiDex-100.github.io/BiDex-100/)
- **[CRONOS: Benchmarking Non-Episodic Reinforcement Learning for Robotic Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.05080)** (2026) — A simulation benchmark for RL fine-tuning of VLAs without frequent resets; finds naive fine-tuning fails from object and end-effector shifts, and that targeted reset allocation and bias fixes mitigate it. · [project](https://embodiedai-ntu.github.io/cronos/)
- **[ClothDojo: Learned Data Generation and Benchmarking for Bimanual Garment Flattening and Folding](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.01190)** (2026) — Sim platform where a fine-tuned VLA generates nearly 8,000 successful garment trajectories, re-rendered for RGB policies. Those beat policies trained on the original human data, but unseen garments stay hard. · [project](https://clothdojo.github.io/)
- **[CrowdMAPS: Crowdsourcing Multiple Actions per State for Real-World Imitation Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.15078)** (2026) — Remote annotators label extra valid actions for already-visited robot states in a web 3D tool, widening datasets without more robot time. 1 → 10 actions per state adds ~33–36 points success on five real tasks.
- **[DexArena: A Unified Benchmark for Long-Horizon Dexterous Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.02629)** (2026) — 10 long-horizon and 30 stage-level bimanual dexterous tasks with physically grounded success conditions per stage, so partial progress is measurable; includes verified teleoperated demos. · [project](https://sipair-elastic.github.io/DexArena-website/)
- **[Diversity You Can Actually Measure: A Fast, Model-Free Diversity Metric for Robotics Datasets](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.11634)** (2026) — CoRL 2026 Oral. Measures diversity of imitation datasets directly from demonstrations, with entropy defined through a signature kernel, so no model has to be trained first.
- **[Do as I Do: Dexterous Manipulation Data from Everyday Human Videos](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.19333)** (2026) — Reconstructs hand-object interaction from ordinary monocular human videos and retargets it into executable multi-fingered robot actions; beats prior work on hand-object estimation and gives a data-collection playbook. · [code](https://github.com/malik-group/do-as-i-do) · [project](https://do-as-i-do.com/)
- **[Ego2Robot: Scalable Robot Data Synthesis from Egocentric Human Data](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.02580)** (2026) — CoRL 2026 Spotlight. A pipeline that turns egocentric human manipulation videos into robot training data, testing whether this helps VLA pretraining at scale. · [project](https://www-ye.github.io/ego2robot_blog/)
- **[EgoContact: Evaluating Physical Intelligence with Egocentric Video](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.39167)** (2026) — Benchmark of 22.5K verified egocentric interactions with pixel-precise contact points: current models often miss where to touch. As a memory bank for an affordance policy it lifts real grasp success from 16% to 58%. · [project](https://egocontact.github.io)
- **[EgoExplorer: A Vision-Motion-Touch Observatory for Human Loco-Manipulation In Situ](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.18872)** (2026) — 100 h of wearable-captured human loco-manipulation (mostly professional work) aligning egocentric video, whole-body and finger motion, touch and step-level text; reports 71.9% lower body error than baselines. · [code](https://github.com/InternRobotics/EgoExplorer) · [project](https://internrobotics.github.io/egoexplorer.github.io/)
- **[EgoHTR: Egocentric 4D Demonstrations of Human Terrain Traversal](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.13472)** (2026) — 55 scene-aligned 4D human motion sequences (150k+ frames) on rough terrain from egocentric wearables and a portable scanner, with an open pipeline; used to train perceptive locomotion run on a Unitree G1. · [code](https://github.com/leggedrobotics/egohtr) · [project](https://egohtr.github.io)
- **[EgoVerse: An Egocentric Human Dataset for Robot Learning from Around the World](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.07607)** (2026) — A shared platform and dataset for human-data robot learning: 1,362 hours and 80k episodes across 1,965 tasks and 2,087 demonstrators, with contributions from labs and industry.
- **[Embodied First Exam](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.12177)** (2026) — Benchmark for coding agents that control robots their own way (direct, code, or training a policy): 1,156 tasks, 35 suites, 47 robots. The best of eight agents solves 34% of the 456 core tasks in the realistic setting. · [code](https://github.com/mll-lab-nu/embodied-first-exam) · [project](https://www.embodied-first-exam.ai/)
- **[Embodied Playground: A Long-Horizon Agentic Robotics Benchmark for Physical, Strategic, and Spatial Reasoning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.27039)** (2026) — Benchmark of 410 long-horizon task environments (manipulation, puzzles, household) for foundation-model robot agents, with a curated EP-100 split and a shared protocol checking goals, rules and physical validity. · [code](https://github.com/Winniechen2002/Embodied-Playground) · [project](https://winniechen2002.github.io/Agent-bench-website/)
- **[Embodied Skill Bench: Benchmarking How Well Agents with Embodied Skills Perform on Physical Tasks](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.16238)** (2026) — Packages VLA policies, perception models and know-how as 250 reusable "embodied skills" and measures how much they help LLM agents on physical tasks. · [code](https://github.com/HanZhi306/EmbodiedSkill-Bench)
- **[EmbodiedLab: Benchmarking Embodied Agents That Act to Know](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.35385)** (2026) — Benchmark where a robot must run physics experiments to find an unknown answer: 30 experiments, 5 disciplines, parameters resampled per instance. Reports even the best frontier agent succeeds under half the time. · [code](https://github.com/HexaFuture/EmbodiedLab) · [project](https://hexafuture.ai/benchmarks/embodiedlab/)
- **[FLORA: An Open Model Suite for Scaling Robotics Data Engines](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31779)** (2026) — The open data engine behind GR00T training: models that write timestamped action captions, dense process rewards and episode outcome rewards for human and robot video; reports SOTA on WGO-Bench, RoboReward and Robometer. · [code](https://github.com/NVIDIA/Flora) · [project](https://research.nvidia.com/labs/gear/flora)
- **[Flex-RSI: Benchmarking Recursive Self-Improvement of Frontier Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.08946)** (2026) — Agents revise their own harness under a fixed budget on vision and robotics tasks; self-improvement helps most when the agent builds its own solution; open-weight models lag most under strict physical checks. · [code](https://github.com/flex-rsi/rsi-bench) · [project](https://flex-rsi.github.io)
- **[FoldNet++: a Large-Scale Synthetic Dataset for Robotic T-Shirt Folding and Unfolding](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.12433)** (2026) — Synthetic T-shirt folding and unfolding data: 6 robots, 1K shirts, 120K episodes. Policies trained only on it are reported above 90% success on unseen real shirts. · [code](https://github.com/GalaxyGeneralRobotics/FoldNetXX) · [project](https://pku-epic.github.io/FoldNetXX/)
- **[H2RBench: A Real-to-Sim Benchmark for Evaluating Human-to-Robot Transfer](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.24778)** (2026) — Real-to-sim benchmark for human-to-robot transfer: real human video demos plus simulated robot demos on four tasks, with a fixed protocol to compare how H2R methods scale with more human data.
- **[HARP-Bench: Benchmarking Robot Manipulation Around Active Humans](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.11123)** (2026) — Manipulation benchmark where simulated humans cue goals, finish parts of the task, interrupt or share the workspace. Reports that VLA and IL policies drop when humans act, and higher success does not mean safer motion. · [code](https://github.com/UMass-Embodied-AGI/harp)
- **[HandsOn: A Large-Scale Simulation Benchmark for Generalizable Dexterous Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.26765)** (2026) — Isaac Sim benchmark with 5 arms, 6 dexterous hands, ~200 assets and 18k human demos over 66 tasks; reports the best trained policy (a WAM) at only 29.1% on Core-50, near 0% on precision-fit and deformable tasks. · [code](https://github.com/TsinghuaC3I/HandsOn) · [project](https://llltttwww.github.io/HandsOn-website/)
- **[HelpMeOut: A Neuropsychology-Grounded Benchmark for Error-Aware Monitoring of Household Task Execution](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.17694)** (2026) — HelpMeOut: 20,000 paired household activity episodes with nine neuropsychology-based error types; agents must spot when a person's task execution goes wrong. Learned, symbolic and LLM monitors all do poorly. · [project](https://whrcn.github.io/HelpMeOut-Official/)
- **[HiPHI: A Large-Scale Benchmark for High-Precision Human Motion and Object-Interaction](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.16222)** (2026) — 600+ hours of whole-body human motion and object interaction from optical mocap with sub-millimetre marker accuracy, organised by FrameNet primitives, with a benchmark suite for humanoid learning. · [project](https://noitom-robotics.github.io/hiphi/)
- **[IM-ENGINE: Image Editing for Embodied Data Generation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.06279)** (2026) — Uses image editing of simulator renders as a middle step: edit in task semantics, recover 3D state from simulator priors, refine in physics, and turn it into robot supervision, e.g. dexterous grasps and goal states.
- **[IMBench: A Benchmark for Intuitive Robotic Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.15641)** (2026) — IMBench: 35 tasks and 14K trajectories testing reasoning and execution together under explicit constraints. VLMs reason partly but fail to plan executable steps; VLAs struggle with the constraints. · [project](https://imbench.org/)
- **[Is It the Curriculum or Just the Easy Start? Benchmarking Unsupervised Environment Design on Quadrupeds](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.40364)** (2026) — Runs fourteen curricula under one protocol on five quadrupeds in MuJoCo MJX: no method tops every robot and level set, and methods with easy training levels lead on in-distribution random terrain. · [code](https://github.com/Solvro/ml-wojtek-ued)
- **[KlaskTron-IL: A Contact-Rich Adversarial Imitation Learning Benchmark with a Validated Sim-to-Real Bridge](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.03091)** (2026) — An imitation-learning benchmark on the open KlaskTron table game robot: 80k real human demo steps, augmented and synthetic data, and baselines exposing BC and GAIL failure modes; policies run on hardware unchanged.
- **[LEASH: Stay Safe in Agentic Robotics](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.14115)** (2026) — RoboRisk: 92 sim + 13 real closed-loop tasks on when a model-driven robot should refuse, replan or stop; six frontier models show high harm. LEASH adds task, action and commit gates that cut harm cheaply. · [code](https://github.com/zhuqinfeng1999/LEASH) · [project](https://zhuqinfeng1999.github.io/LEASH/)
- **[LIBERO-Industry: Benchmarking Robotic Manipulation under Industrial Process Constraints](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.16782)** (2026) — 216 LIBERO-based tasks with industrial constraints — conveyor loading/unloading/transfer, speed, time windows, tight tolerances; reports that VLAs and world action models degrade as constraints are combined. · [code](https://github.com/IAIE-PhysicalAI/LIBERO-Industry) · [project](https://iaie-physicalai.github.io/LIBERO-Industry/)
- **[MESA: An Evaluation Framework for Compositional, Semantic, and Spatial Generalization in Robotics](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.05567)** (2026) — Suites isolate generalisation across spatial layout, object instance, object category and subtask composition, plus bimanual BiMESA. - **[MolmoSpaces-CommonSense: Evaluating Commonsense in Robotic Manipulation Policies](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.41377)** (2026) — Benchmark built on a taxonomy of commonsense reasoning (occlusion, containment, affordances). Reports zero-shot policies dropping over 50% as distractors grow and acting on the wrong object 30% of the time in clutter. · [code](https://github.com/allenai/molmospaces)
- **[Monkey See, Can Monkey Do? A Benchmark for Evaluating Robot Skill Learning by Observation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.08209)** (2026) — RoboReel: a unified benchmark for policies learned from human videos, bundling real human demos, simulated robot trajectories and ten tasks with four test suites; sim trends hold on real-robot recreations. · [project](https://roboreel.github.io)
- **[NavVerse: Benchmarking Indoor-to-Outdoor Embodied Navigation in Continuous Robot Simulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.19695)** (2026) — Physics-based benchmark for navigating from buildings to streets in one episode: 200 scenes, 10,000 ObjectNav / VLN / PlaceNav episodes. Zero-shot VLAs succeed most; the modular method is safest.
- **[OmniPiano: Diverse Dexterous Piano-Playing Challenges for Standard, Robust, Safe, and Multi-Agent RL](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.00243)** (2026) — Extends robot piano to one to five Shadow Hands, with robust, safe and multi-agent RL settings: at least 912 task settings and 36 baselines. - **[PyRUA-DataFarm: LLM Agent as a Cost-Efficient Teleoperator for Real-Ready Robot Data](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.05320)** (2026) — A coding agent rebuilds a task in simulation from one calibration episode, then a video model renders photographic data; reports 93 vs. 84 of 100 real successes against teleop data, at about $107 per hour of data. · [code](https://github.com/DAGroup-PKU/PyRUA-Lean) · [project](https://dagroup-pku.github.io/PyRUA-Lean/DataFarm)

- **[RoboCasa-Shift: Stage-Aware Robustness Evaluation of Vision-Language-Action Policies in Long-Horizon Household Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.27861)** (2026) — VLA robustness benchmark on RoboCasa365: paired clean/perturbed rollouts differing in one shift, plus stage-level diagnostics of where long-horizon failures start; evaluated on GR00T N1.5. · [code](https://github.com/HaochengWang23/RoboCasa-Shift)
- **[RoboFollow: Unveiling the Instruction Following Mirage in Embodied Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.25636)** (2026) — Benchmark showing VLA success often hides weak instruction following: scenes allow several valid tasks so language matters. - **[RoboGene: Diversity-Driven Agentic Task Generation Framework for VLA Pre-training](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.16444)** (2026) — Agentic generator of diverse, feasible manipulation tasks for data collection, with self-reflection and human-verified memory; 18k real trajectories collected. - **[RoboJev: An Open Vision-Language Judge for Robot Success Detection](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.33718)** (2026) — An open-weight model that judges from video frames whether a manipulation attempt succeeded, useful for labeling episodes; reports 0.89 AUROC on unseen labs and robots. · [project](https://robo-jev.com)
- **[RoboJudge: Multimodal Language Models as Judges for Embodied Video Generation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.27782)** (2026) — Benchmark of 800 human-rated robot/hand videos from 10 generators to test MLLM judges on physical adherence and instruction alignment, plus a 12K-video training set and RoboJudge-9B (Pearson 0.719 with humans). · [code](https://github.com/Sphere-AI-Lab/RoboJudge) · [project](https://spherelab.ai/RoboJudge/)

- **[RoboPocket: Improve Robot Policies Instantly with Your Phone](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.05504)** (2026) — Smartphone app for robot-free policy iteration: shows the current policy's predicted trajectory in AR so users collect corrective data where it fails. - **[RoboValue: A Fine-Grained Sim-and-Real Benchmark for Unified Evaluation of Robotic Value Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.26663)** (2026) — Tests robot value (progress) models on 15 sim and 20 real tasks across task state, progress, failure/recovery and consistency; reports that good forward-progress scores hide weak history and recovery reasoning. · [code](https://github.com/RoboValue-Benchmark/RoboValue) · [project](https://robovalue-benchmark.github.io/)
- **[RoboWM-Bench: A Benchmark for Evaluating World Models in Robotic Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.19092)** (2026) — Evaluates video world models by whether generated manipulation can be converted into actions and executed in physics simulation, not only whether it looks realistic.
- **[RobotAgentBench: Towards Open-world Robot Autonomy](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.34649)** (2026) — Benchmark for open-world robot agents: 50 household layouts built real-to-sim, tasks from 90 atomic ones to 4 open-ended scenarios, scoring discovery, reasoning, manipulation, recovery and robustness. · [code](https://github.com/AetherLabsAI/RobotAgentBench) · [project](https://aetherlabsai.github.io/RobotAgentBench/)
- **[Rollout then Recycle: 3,718 Labelled Real-Robot Episodes from Benchmarking 7 Policies on 12 Tasks](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.03168)** (2026) — Releases every episode of the ArmnetBench v0.1 evaluation on SO-101 arms: 600 teleoperated demos and 3,118 labelled policy rollouts, 73% of them failures, so many policies' mistakes can be compared on the same tasks.
- **[SO-101 Bench: Accessible Spatial Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.36420)** (2026) — Isaac Lab digital-twin benchmark for language-conditioned spatial tasks on the low-cost SO-101 arm (138 objects, 916 episodes); sim ranks policies exactly as the real arm. Best VLAs ~61%, an LLM-agent harness 83.2%. · [code](https://github.com/5hadytru/so101_bench) · [project](https://so101-bench.github.io/)
- **[SPARC: Reliable Spatial Annotations from Robot Demonstrations at Scale](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.13497)** (2026) — Auto-labels robot demos with boxes, object trajectories and phase labels, plus a reliability score from the task's spatio-temporal structure, so fewer noisy labels are kept and fewer good ones are thrown out.
- **[Scalable Behavior Cloning with Open Data, Training, and Evaluation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.27375)** (2026) — Fully open behavior-cloning stack: ABC-130K (3,500 hours, 130K episodes, 195 tasks), hardware, training code and a sim co-training recipe whose sim scores track real-world results. · [code](https://github.com/amazon-far/abc) · [project](https://abc.bot)
- **[The Imitator Game: Benchmarking Robot Imitative Ability Beyond Action Prediction](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.22301)** (2026) — Four-level benchmark that widens the gap between a human demo and the robot's scene, plus IG-10K (20,000+ paired human-robot episodes). Nine models collapse at the hardest level; all stay below 13% zero-shot. · [code](https://github.com/imitator-game/The-Imitator-Game) · [project](https://imitator-game.github.io)
- **[ToolDex: An Ego-Exo Human Demonstration Dataset for Creative Physical Tool Use](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.09036)** (2026) — Human demos of creative tool use (repurposing objects when the usual tool is missing) from 1 ego + 8 exo cameras with hand, tool and object labels; benchmarks reasoning, 3D/4D reconstruction and policy learning. · [project](https://tooluse-dataset.github.io/)
- **[Vision-Language-Action in Robotics: A Survey of Datasets, Benchmarks, and Data Engines](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.23001)** (2026) — Argues VLA progress now depends more on data engines and evaluation than on architectures; finds a fidelity-versus-cost trade-off in datasets and gaps in compositional and long-horizon benchmarks. · [project](https://github.com/ziyaow1010/vla-datasets-benchmarks)
- **[YUBI: Yielding Universal Bidigital Interface for Bimanual Dexterous Manipulation at Scale](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.10244)** (2026) — Finger-driven handheld gripper for UMI-style data collection plus an 8,434-hour, 1.20M-episode, 119-task dataset; one policy transfers across UR, Franka and ELEY by mounting the gripper. Hardware and data released. · [code](https://github.com/airoa-org/yubi-sw) · [project](https://yubi.airoa.io/)
- **[AgiBot World Colosseo: A Large-scale Manipulation Platform for Scalable and Intelligent Embodied Systems](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.06669)** (2025) — A million-scale real-robot dataset collected in purpose-built scenes, with the GO-1 generalist policy trained on it.
- **[Can AI Perceive Physical Danger and Intervene?](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.21651)** (2025) — ASIMOV v2: physical-safety benchmark built from real injury narratives turned into generated images and videos, testing whether foundation models notice danger and intervene, plus safety-reasoning post-training. · [project](https://asimov-benchmark.github.io/v2)
- **[EgoDex: Learning Dexterous Manipulation from Large-Scale Egocentric Video](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.11709)** (2025) — 829 hours of egocentric Apple Vision Pro video with tracked 3D poses for every hand joint, across 194 tabletop tasks from tying shoelaces to folding laundry; includes hand-trajectory prediction benchmarks. · [code](https://github.com/apple-aiml-research/ml-egodex)
- **[PHUMA: Physically Reliable Humanoid Locomotion Dataset](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.26236)** (2025) — CoRL 2026 Spotlight. Humanoid locomotion dataset from internet video that targets the floating, penetration and foot skating common in earlier video-derived data. · [code](https://github.com/davian-robotics/PHUMA) · [project](https://davian-robotics.github.io/PHUMA)
- **[RoboChallenge: Large-scale Real-robot Evaluation of Embodied Policies](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.17950)** (2025) — Report on an online system for testing many robot policies on many real tasks, plus a study of recent VLAs on its first benchmark, Table30.
- **[TWIST2: Scalable, Portable, and Holistic Humanoid Data Collection System](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.02832)** (2025) — Portable, mocap-free humanoid teleoperation and data collection built around a VR headset, aimed at making whole-body demonstrations scale. · [code](https://github.com/amazon-far/TWIST2) · [project](https://twist-data.github.io)
- **[DROID: A Large-Scale In-The-Wild Robot Manipulation Dataset](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.12945)** (2024) — 76k teleoperated Franka trajectories collected in hundreds of real scenes across many institutions. · [code](https://github.com/droid-dataset/droid)
- **[HumanoidBench: Simulated Humanoid Benchmark for Whole-Body Locomotion and Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.10506)** (2024) — 27 simulated tasks (12 locomotion, 15 whole-body manipulation) on a Unitree H1 with two dexterous hands; reports that DreamerV3, TD-MPC2, SAC and PPO fail most of them, especially long-horizon whole-body ones. · [code](https://github.com/carlosferrazza/humanoid-bench) · [project](https://humanoid-bench.github.io)
- **[Point Cloud Matters: Rethinking the Impact of Different Observation Spaces on Robot Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.02500)** (2024) — OBSBench compares RGB, RGB-D and point cloud observations for robot learning under one setup. · [code](https://github.com/HaoyiZhu/PointCloudMatters)
- **[RoboMIND: Benchmark on Multi-embodiment Intelligence Normative Data for Robot Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.13877)** (2024) — Large real-robot teleoperation dataset across several embodiments (single-arm, dual-arm, humanoid) collected under one standardized protocol, and it keeps failed demos with annotated causes. · [project](https://x-humanoid-robomind.github.io/)
- **[THE COLOSSEUM: A Benchmark for Evaluating Generalization for Robotic Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.08191)** (2024) — 20 RLBench tasks × 14 perturbation factors (color, texture, lighting, distractors, camera pose, friction, mass…); reports 30–50% success drops for leading policies, 75%+ when perturbations combine. · [code](https://github.com/robot-colosseum/robot-colosseum) · [project](https://robot-colosseum.github.io)
- **[VLABench: A Large-Scale Benchmark for Language-Conditioned Robotics Manipulation with Long-Horizon Reasoning Tasks](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.18194)** (2024) — 100 task categories over 2,000+ objects that need common sense, world knowledge and multi-step reasoning, not just instruction following; evaluates VLAs, embodied agents and VLMs in one suite. · [code](https://github.com/OpenMOSS/VLABench) · [project](https://vlabench.github.io/)
- **[ARNOLD: A Benchmark for Language-Grounded Task Learning With Continuous States in Realistic 3D Scenes](https://arxiv.org/abs/2304.04321)** (2023) — Benchmark for language-grounded tasks with continuous object goal states in realistic 3D scenes, where most suites assume binary goals. · [code](https://github.com/arnold-benchmark/arnold) · [project](https://arnold-benchmark.github.io)
- **[BridgeData V2: A Dataset for Robot Learning at Scale](https://arxiv.org/abs/2308.12952)** (2023) — 60k trajectories on a low-cost WidowX arm across many kitchens and tasks; a common pretraining source.
- **[Ego-Exo4D: Understanding Skilled Human Activity from First- and Third-Person Perspectives](https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.18259)** (2023) — Time-synced first-person and third-person video of people performing skilled activities (cooking, sports, repair...), with rich annotations; a large source for learning manipulation skills from humans.
- **[RH20T: A Comprehensive Robotic Dataset for Learning Diverse Skills in One-Shot](https://arxiv.org/abs/2307.00595)** (2023) — 110k+ contact-rich sequences with vision, force, audio and paired human demonstration videos.
- **[ARCTIC: A Dataset for Dexterous Bimanual Hand-Object Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2204.13662)** (2022) — 2.1M video frames of two hands manipulating articulated objects (scissors, laptops…) with accurate 3D hand and object meshes from motion capture; a reference for bimanual hand-object reconstruction. · [code](https://github.com/zc-alexfan/arctic)
- **[HOI4D: A 4D Egocentric Dataset for Category-Level Human-Object Interaction](https://arxiv.org/abs/2203.01577)** (2022) — 2.4M egocentric RGB-D frames (4,000+ sequences, 800 object instances in 16 categories, 610 rooms) of people handling rigid and articulated objects, with segmentation, hand and object pose labels. · [code](https://github.com/leolyliu/HOI4D-Instructions)
- **[OakInk: A Large-scale Knowledge Repository for Understanding Hand-Object Interaction](https://arxiv.org/abs/2203.15709)** (2022) — Affordance labels for ~1,800 household objects plus 50K intent-oriented hand-object interactions recorded on 100 of them; a common source for dexterous grasp synthesis. Follow-up OakInk2 covers bimanual tasks. · [code](https://github.com/oakink/OakInk)
- **[Objaverse: A Universe of Annotated 3D Objects](https://arxiv.org/abs/2212.08051)** (2022) — 800K+ annotated 3D object models; widely used to populate simulators with diverse objects for grasping, rearrangement and procedural scene generation (Objaverse-XL extends it to 10M+).
- **[DexYCB: A Benchmark for Capturing Hand Grasping of Objects](https://arxiv.org/abs/2104.04631)** (2021) — ~582K RGB-D frames (1,000 sequences, 10 people, 20 YCB objects, 8 cameras) of human grasps with hand and object pose labels; benchmarks detection, 6D object pose, 3D hand pose and human-to-robot handover. · [code](https://github.com/NVlabs/dex-ycb-toolkit)
- **[Ego4D: Around the World in 3,000 Hours of Egocentric Video](https://arxiv.org/abs/2110.07058)** (2021) — 3,670 hours of first-person daily-life video; widely used to pretrain visual representations for robots.
- **[Habitat-Matterport 3D Dataset (HM3D): 1000 Large-scale 3D Environments for Embodied AI](https://arxiv.org/abs/2109.08238)** (2021) — 1,000 3D scans of real indoor spaces for Habitat; the main scene set behind ObjectNav and many embodied navigation benchmarks. · [code](https://github.com/facebookresearch/habitat-matterport3d-dataset)
- **[Rescaling Egocentric Vision](https://arxiv.org/abs/2006.13256)** (2020) — EPIC-KITCHENS-100 — 100 hours (20M frames, ~90K action segments) of unscripted first-person kitchen video from 45 environments, with dense action labels and challenges such as action anticipation. · [code](https://github.com/epic-kitchens/epic-kitchens-100-annotations)
- **[RoboNet: Large-Scale Multi-Robot Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/1910.11215)** (2019) — An early shared dataset of robot interaction videos from seven robot platforms, used to pretrain visual dynamics models that transfer to new robots - a forerunner of Open X-Embodiment. · [code](https://github.com/SudeepDasari/RoboNet)
- **[Benchmarking in Manipulation Research: The YCB Object and Model Set and Benchmarking Protocols](https://arxiv.org/abs/1502.03143)** (2015) — The YCB object set — 73 everyday objects with scans, textured meshes and simulation models, plus benchmarking protocols; widely used in grasping and pose-estimation work.

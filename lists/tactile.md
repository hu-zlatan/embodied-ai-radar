- ⭐⭐⭐ **[DIGIT: A Novel Design for a Low-Cost Compact High-Resolution Tactile Sensor with Application to In-Hand Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2005.14679)** (2020) — An inexpensive, compact GelSight-style fingertip sensor that made vision-based touch widely accessible. · [code](https://github.com/facebookresearch/digit-interface)
- ⭐ **[Visible Touch: Rendering Contact for Visuomotor Policies](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.14156)** (2026) — CoRL 2026 Spotlight. Brings contact into image-conditioned policies by rendering it, targeting the gap left by approaches that need tactile hardware, separate tactile encoders or non-image backbones. · [code](https://github.com/qaz1678249/visibletouch) · [project](https://visibletouch.github.io/)
- **[$TF^2$-Steering: Action Refinement with Tactile Forecasts and Feedback via Velocity-Field Steering](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.01483)** (2026) — Keeps a vision-based flow-matching policy and learns to steer its velocity field from tactile forecasts and live feedback, so touch can correct actions. Reports gains over baselines in six sim and six real tasks. · [project](https://yiming-cc.github.io/tf2-steering/)
- **[ACTS: Learning Visuo-Tactile World Models from Human Physical Interaction](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.24285)** (2026) — Visuo-tactile world model trained only on humans handling GelSight sensors (4.9 h React dataset). Reports that contact dynamics transfer to robot rollouts more reliably than visual appearance does. · [project](https://acts-anon.github.io/)
- **[DeTac: Dual-branch Decoupling of Texture and Force Signals in Vision-Based Tactile Sensors](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.16463)** (2026) — CoRL 2026 Spotlight. Dual-branch generative framework that extracts clean shading and marker images at once from one marker-based tactile frame, avoiding serial mask-based inpainting.
- **[Equivariant Visual-Tactile Diffusion Policy for Contact-Rich Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.03333)** (2026) — VISTA: equivariant visuotactile diffusion policy that fuses touch into spherical visual tokens and rotates them with the gripper pose, for more data-efficient contact-rich imitation. · [code](https://github.com/Kenn3o3/Vista) · [project](https://vista-paper.github.io/)
- **[Evaluating Tactile Foundation Models for End-to-End Imitation Learning in Contact-Rich Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.00957)** (2026) — Plugs several pretrained tactile foundation models into Diffusion Policy and compares them in one setup, including fine-tuning and mid-training strategies, with over 5,000 real-world rollouts. · [code](https://github.com/isri-aist/TacLatentIL) · [project](https://mmurooka.github.io/tfm4il-project-page/)
- **[FACTR 2: Learning External Force Sensing for Commodity Robot Arms Improves Policy Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.12406)** (2026) — CoRL 2026 Oral. NEXT estimates external joint torques without force sensors, training in 1 minute from 10 minutes of free-motion data, enabling force-feedback teleoperation and better policy learning on low-cost arms. · [code](https://github.com/philiphan0109/factr2_next) · [project](https://jasonjzliu.com/factr2)
- **[FM-VLA: Force-based Memory for Vision-Language-Action Models in Contact-Rich Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.18231)** (2026) — Gives a VLA memory through compact force-history tokens from a pretrained VAE, for tasks like pressing a button a set number of times. Reports over 80% success on three memory-dependent tasks. · [project](https://qft-333.github.io/FM-VLA-Page/)
- **[FTP-1: A Generalist Foundation Tactile Policy Across Tactile Sensors for Contact-Rich Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.13102)** (2026) — Tactile policy pretrained on ~3,000 hours from 26 sources and 21 sensors, mapping image, array and state signals into shared tokens. Reports +17.2% on seen setups and +31% on two unseen sensor setups. · [project](https://ftp1-policy.github.io)
- **[FingerEye: Learning Dexterous Manipulation with Continuous Vision-Tactile Sensing](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.20689)** (2026) — CoRL 2026 Spotlight. Fingertip sensing that combines binocular RGB cameras with a compliant contact surface, giving feedback from pre-contact approach through post-contact control. · [code](https://github.com/ariszxxu/FingerEye) · [project](https://nus-lins-lab.github.io/FingerEyeWeb/)
- **[ForceBand: Learning Forceful Manipulation with sEMG](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.26093)** (2026) — Low-cost wrist sEMG band whose model predicts per-finger forces, turning video demos into force-labelled ones after a short calibration. Reports >50% lower force error than vision and 87% on force-sensitive tasks. · [project](https://forceband-emg.github.io)
- **[GelSlim 5.0: Unifying Vision and Touch with a Single Fingertip Camera](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31334)** (2026) — Fingertip sensor whose one camera switches illumination to alternate between see-through-membrane vision and tactile images — one shared viewpoint, no extrinsic calibration; fused by a transformer (VisTacFusion).
- **[Heterogeneous Tactile Transformer](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.29948)** (2026) — Shared tactile representation across sensors: sensor-specific encoders plus a common transformer, pretrained on a new 1.6M-frame paired dataset from four sensors; adapts to new tasks and unseen sensors. · [project](https://jxbi1010.github.io/htt-gh-page/)
- **[Inference-time Policy Steering via Vision and Touch](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.14981)** (2026) — Inference-time steering with vision and touch: visual sampling picks the behaviour, tactile-guided diffusion editing fixes local contact, scored by a visuo-tactile latent world model. Reports +51% over the base policy. · [project](https://yilin-wu98.github.io/vital_website)
- **[Learning with Object-centric Representations of Tactile Interactive Perception for Robot Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.33235)** (2026) — Learns object representations from tactile exploration, aligned with text descriptions of properties, and uses them as context for manipulation. Reports task success from 41% to 92% (seen) and 19% to 67% (unseen). · [project](https://xinyiyxyx.github.io/tactile-object-centric/)
- **[Modular Sensory Stream for Integrating Physical Feedback in Vision-Language-Action Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.23272)** (2026) — Adds separate tactile, force and torque streams to a pretrained VLA through joint cross-modal attention, with staged training and an auxiliary task that predicts future physical signals. Real-robot tests. · [project](https://jiminlx.github.io/MoSS)
- **[Multi-Resolution Tactile Imitation Learning for Contact-Rich Robotic Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.06281)** (2026) — Fuses an RGB camera, a GelSight Mini and a high-rate event-based tactile sensor for a flow-matching policy. Reports 80% average success on five contact-rich tasks vs 35.5% vision-only. · [project](https://mitas-touch.github.io)
- **[Multisensory Continual Learning: Adapting Pretrained Visuomotor Policies to Force](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.30988)** (2026) — Adds force-torque sensing to a pretrained vision-only world-action model with staged fusion, multisensory future prediction and replay of pretraining data; strong on contact tasks without losing original skills. · [project](https://jadenvc.github.io/multisensory-continual-learning/)
- **[OmniTacTune: Policy-Agnostic Real-World RL for Tactile Residual Adaptation of Visual Policies](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.03723)** (2026) — Real-world RL that adds a lightweight tactile residual on top of any pretrained visual policy, bootstrapped from the base policy's own rollouts. Reports 5–40% success raised to 85–100% within 40–80 minutes. · [project](https://colinyu1.github.io/omnitactune-site/)
- **[OpenTac: An Open Ecosystem for Customizable, Scalable, and Transferable 3D Tactile Sensing](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31661)** (2026) — Open-source resistive tactile skin whose structured overlay turns shear into pressure patterns, so neighbouring taxels decode shear and normal force; ships with a design tool for custom skins plus hardware and software. · [code](https://github.com/opentac/opentac) · [project](https://opentac.github.io)
- **[PACE: Temporal-Role Tactile Conditioning for Chunk-Based Insertion Policies](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.16308)** (2026) — CoRL 2026 Oral. Splits one tactile stream into three temporal roles, persistent states, abrupt contact events and future consequences, instead of a single fusion path in chunk-based insertion policies. · [project](https://pace-insertion.github.io/)
- **[PTLD: Sim-to-real Privileged Tactile Latent Distillation for Dexterous Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.04531)** (2026) — Learns tactile dexterous skills without tactile simulation: privileged sensors in the real world collect data to distil a tactile state estimator for a sim-trained policy. - **[PanoTac: A Dense Three-Axis Tactile Sensor for Robust Touch-Centric Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.03520)** (2026) — A compliant fingertip under $50 gives a dense 12×32 normal and shear map at 60 Hz. Visuo-tactile policies beat vision-only baselines by 46.7 points on average across scooping, pouring and test-tube tasks. · [project](https://panotac.github.io)
- **[ReflexArc: Bottom-Up Integration of Instantaneous Tactile Reflexes into Whole-Body Humanoid Control](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.04424)** (2026) — Adds a full-body tactile reflex that reaches the motors within 10 ms beneath a learned whole-body policy; on a G1 it cuts first force peak 34.1% versus the same reflex delayed by 20 ms. · [project](https://minusmodulo.github.io/reflexarc-website/)
- **[T-Rex: Tactile-Reactive Dexterous Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.17055)** (2026) — Tactile-reactive VLA: 100-hour tactile dataset built around motor primitives, a variable-rate mixture-of-transformers and a temporal tactile VQ-VAE. - **[TacCoRL: Integrating Tactile Feedback into VLA via Simulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.11743)** (2026) — Adds touch to a VLA via sim-real co-training and simulated RL in a real-aligned simulator, learning how contact should change actions near failure. - **[TacMind: Benchmarking and Modeling Tactile Interaction States for Robotic Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.00455)** (2026) — 128 simulated tactile tasks in three regimes (Precise, Recognition, Dynamic) with 12 perturbations, plus a tactile world model that treats touch as an evolving state and refines actions with predicted contact. · [code](https://github.com/Tacmind-Bench/TacMind) · [project](https://tacmind-bench.github.io/)
- **[TacStyle: Personalizing Tactile Robot Policies using Structured Behavior Representations](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.14862)** (2026) — Learns a structured latent space of behaviour styles (e.g. how much force to apply), and lets language reason over it instead of generating actions directly, for personalized tactile policies like wiping or folding.
- **[TactX: Learning Shared Tactile Representations Across Diverse Sensors](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.31236)** (2026) — Encoders for resistive, magnetic and vision-based tactile sensors are trained on paired contacts into one latent space, so a policy trained with one sensor transfers zero-shot to another on four manipulation tasks.
- **[Tactile Curiosity Drives Robot Interaction](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.40134)** (2026) — TacEx points curiosity at touch: it splits model uncertainty by sense so exploration seeks contact, not free-space motion. - **[Tactile-Based Robotic Manipulation: From Mechanics-Driven to Data-Driven Perspectives](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30438)** (2026) — Review (Annual Review of Control, Robotics, and Autonomous Systems) tracing tactile manipulation from contact-mechanics models to learned policies: sensing basics, state estimation, IL, RL, VLAs, predictive models.
- **[TactileStep: Sole Tactile Learning for Regulating Foot-Terrain Interaction in Humanoid Locomotion](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.28959)** (2026) — CoRL 2026 Spotlight. - **[FACTR: Force-Attending Curriculum Training for Contact-Rich Policy Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.17432)** (2025) — Low-cost bilateral teleop relays arm forces to the operator, and a curriculum corrupts vision less and less over training so policies learn to attend to force.
- **[Learning to Feel the Future: DreamTacVLA for Contact-Rich Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.23864)** (2025) — DreamTacVLA feeds high-res tactile images with wrist and third-person views, aligns them spatially, then fine-tunes with a tactile world model that predicts future touch signals.
- **[LocoTouch: Learning Dynamic Quadrupedal Transport with Tactile Sensing](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.23175)** (2025) — A high-density tactile sensor across a quadruped's back lets it carry unsecured cylinders over long distances, a task that usually needs mounting hardware. · [code](https://github.com/linchangyi1/LocoTouch)

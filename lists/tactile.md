- ⭐⭐⭐ **[DIGIT: A Novel Design for a Low-Cost Compact High-Resolution Tactile Sensor with Application to In-Hand Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2005.14679)** (2020) — An inexpensive, compact GelSight-style fingertip sensor that made vision-based touch widely accessible. · [code](https://github.com/facebookresearch/digit-interface)
- ⭐ **[Visible Touch: Rendering Contact for Visuomotor Policies](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.14156)** (2026) — CoRL 2026 Spotlight. Brings contact into image-conditioned policies by rendering it, targeting the gap left by approaches that need tactile hardware, separate tactile encoders or non-image backbones. · [code](https://github.com/qaz1678249/visibletouch) · [project](https://visibletouch.github.io/)
- **[$TF^2$-Steering: Action Refinement with Tactile Forecasts and Feedback via Velocity-Field Steering](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.01483)** (2026) — Keeps a vision-based flow-matching policy and learns to steer its velocity field from tactile forecasts and live feedback, so touch can correct actions. Reports gains over baselines in six sim and six real tasks. · [project](https://yiming-cc.github.io/tf2-steering/)
- **[ACTS: Learning Visuo-Tactile World Models from Human Physical Interaction](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.24285)** (2026) — Visuo-tactile world model trained only on humans handling GelSight sensors (4.9 h React dataset). Reports that contact dynamics transfer to robot rollouts more reliably than visual appearance does. · [project](https://acts-anon.github.io/)
- **[DeTac: Dual-branch Decoupling of Texture and Force Signals in Vision-Based Tactile Sensors](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.16463)** (2026) — CoRL 2026 Spotlight. Dual-branch generative framework that extracts clean shading and marker images at once from one marker-based tactile frame, avoiding serial mask-based inpainting.
- **[Equivariant Visual-Tactile Diffusion Policy for Contact-Rich Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.03333)** (2026) — VISTA: equivariant visuotactile diffusion policy that fuses touch into spherical visual tokens and rotates them with the gripper pose, for more data-efficient contact-rich imitation. · [code](https://github.com/Kenn3o3/Vista) · [project](https://vista-paper.github.io/)
- **[Evaluating Tactile Foundation Models for End-to-End Imitation Learning in Contact-Rich Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.00957)** (2026) — Plugs several pretrained tactile foundation models into Diffusion Policy and compares them in one setup, including fine-tuning and mid-training strategies, with over 5,000 real-world rollouts. · [code](https://github.com/isri-aist/TacLatentIL) · [project](https://mmurooka.github.io/tfm4il-project-page/)
- **[FACTR 2: Learning External Force Sensing for Commodity Robot Arms Improves Policy Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.12406)** (2026) — CoRL 2026 Oral. NEXT estimates external joint torques without force sensors, training in 1 minute from 10 minutes of free-motion data, enabling force-feedback teleoperation and better policy learning on low-cost arms. · [code](https://github.com/philiphan0109/factr2_next) · [project](https://jasonjzliu.com/factr2)
- **[FingerEye: Learning Dexterous Manipulation with Continuous Vision-Tactile Sensing](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.20689)** (2026) — CoRL 2026 Spotlight. Fingertip sensing that combines binocular RGB cameras with a compliant contact surface, giving feedback from pre-contact approach through post-contact control. · [code](https://github.com/ariszxxu/FingerEye) · [project](https://nus-lins-lab.github.io/FingerEyeWeb/)
- **[GelSlim 5.0: Unifying Vision and Touch with a Single Fingertip Camera](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31334)** (2026) — Fingertip sensor whose one camera switches illumination to alternate between see-through-membrane vision and tactile images — one shared viewpoint, no extrinsic calibration; fused by a transformer (VisTacFusion).
- **[OpenTac: An Open Ecosystem for Customizable, Scalable, and Transferable 3D Tactile Sensing](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31661)** (2026) — Open-source resistive tactile skin whose structured overlay turns shear into pressure patterns, so neighbouring taxels decode shear and normal force; ships with a design tool for custom skins plus hardware and software. · [code](https://github.com/opentac/opentac) · [project](https://opentac.github.io)
- **[PACE: Temporal-Role Tactile Conditioning for Chunk-Based Insertion Policies](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.16308)** (2026) — CoRL 2026 Oral. Splits one tactile stream into three temporal roles, persistent states, abrupt contact events and future consequences, instead of a single fusion path in chunk-based insertion policies. · [project](https://pace-insertion.github.io/)
- **[PanoTac: A Dense Three-Axis Tactile Sensor for Robust Touch-Centric Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.03520)** (2026) — A compliant fingertip under $50 gives a dense 12×32 normal and shear map at 60 Hz. Visuo-tactile policies beat vision-only baselines by 46.7 points on average across scooping, pouring and test-tube tasks. · [project](https://panotac.github.io)
- **[ReflexArc: Bottom-Up Integration of Instantaneous Tactile Reflexes into Whole-Body Humanoid Control](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.04424)** (2026) — Adds a full-body tactile reflex that reaches the motors within 10 ms beneath a learned whole-body policy; on a G1 it cuts first force peak 34.1% versus the same reflex delayed by 20 ms. · [project](https://minusmodulo.github.io/reflexarc-website/)
- **[TacMind: Benchmarking and Modeling Tactile Interaction States for Robotic Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.00455)** (2026) — 128 simulated tactile tasks in three regimes (Precise, Recognition, Dynamic) with 12 perturbations, plus a tactile world model that treats touch as an evolving state and refines actions with predicted contact. · [code](https://github.com/Tacmind-Bench/TacMind) · [project](https://tacmind-bench.github.io/)
- **[Tactile Curiosity Drives Robot Interaction](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.40134)** (2026) — TacEx points curiosity at touch: it splits model uncertainty by sense so exploration seeks contact, not free-space motion. The collected data trains pick-and-place offline and post-trains VLAs. · [project](https://go.klem.nz/tacex)
- **[Tactile-Based Robotic Manipulation: From Mechanics-Driven to Data-Driven Perspectives](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30438)** (2026) — Review (Annual Review of Control, Robotics, and Autonomous Systems) tracing tactile manipulation from contact-mechanics models to learned policies: sensing basics, state estimation, IL, RL, VLAs, predictive models.
- **[TactileStep: Sole Tactile Learning for Regulating Foot-Terrain Interaction in Humanoid Locomotion](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.28959)** (2026) — CoRL 2026 Spotlight. Brings sole pressure sensing into humanoid locomotion control, aiming for softer foot-terrain contact than parkour policies that succeed despite harsh landings.
- **[VecTile: A Low-Cost Flexible Tactile Array for 3-DoF Force Sensing](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31472)** (2026) — 6 mm flexible array: silicone nubs over a piezoresistive layer turn shear into readable pressure patterns, giving 3-axis force per taxel; under 35 USD for 4x4. Reports it helps a Diffusion Policy insert a plug. · [project](https://jiannnh.github.io/VecTile/)
- **[AnyTouch: Learning Unified Static-Dynamic Representation across Multiple Visuo-tactile Sensors](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.12191)** (2025) — Learns one static-and-dynamic representation across different visuo-tactile sensors, and contributes TacQuad, an aligned multi-sensor tactile dataset. · [code](https://github.com/GeWu-Lab/AnyTouch)
- **[FACTR: Force-Attending Curriculum Training for Contact-Rich Policy Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.17432)** (2025) — Low-cost bilateral teleop relays arm forces to the operator, and a curriculum corrupts vision less and less over training so policies learn to attend to force.
- **[LocoTouch: Learning Dynamic Quadrupedal Transport with Tactile Sensing](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.23175)** (2025) — A high-density tactile sensor across a quadruped's back lets it carry unsecured cylinders over long distances, a task that usually needs mounting hardware. · [code](https://github.com/linchangyi1/LocoTouch)
- **[PP-Tac: Paper Picking Using Tactile Feedback in Dexterous Robotic Hands](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.16649)** (2025) — Picks up paper-like thin objects with a multi-fingered hand carrying high-resolution omnidirectional tactile sensors, tackling state estimation and grasp planning.
- **[Reactive Diffusion Policy: Slow-Fast Visual-Tactile Policy Learning for Contact-Rich Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.02881)** (2025) — A slow latent diffusion policy plans action chunks while a fast policy reacts to tactile/force feedback; reports >35% higher scores than visual imitation baselines on three contact-rich tasks. · [code](https://github.com/xiaoxiaoxh/reactive_diffusion_policy) · [project](https://reactive-diffusion-policy.github.io/)
- **[Tactile Robotics: An Outlook](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.11261)** (2025) — A broad outlook on tactile robotics, covering piezoresistive, piezoelectric, capacitive, magnetic and optical sensors and where the field is heading.
- **[3D-ViTac: Learning Fine-Grained Manipulation with Visuo-Tactile Sensing](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.24091)** (2024) — Fuses dense tactile arrays with 3D vision in a diffusion policy for fragile, contact-rich bimanual tasks.
- **[A Touch, Vision, and Language Dataset for Multimodal Alignment](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.13232)** (2024) — TVL pairs tactile and visual observations with language descriptions and trains a touch-vision-language encoder plus a model that describes how things feel. · [code](https://github.com/Max-Fu/tvl)
- **[AnySkin: Plug-and-play Skin Sensing for Robotic Touch](https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.08276)** (2024) — A replaceable magnetic tactile skin whose learned policies keep working after the skin is swapped.
- **[Binding Touch to Everything: Learning Unified Multimodal Tactile Representations](https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.18084)** (2024) — UniTouch aligns tactile signals with vision, language and audio embeddings, enabling zero-shot touch understanding.
- **[Digitizing Touch with an Artificial Multimodal Fingertip](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.02479)** (2024) — Digit 360: an open-design, finger-shaped sensor that senses force, fine texture, vibration and heat, with an on-board neural accelerator for reflex-like local processing. · [code](https://github.com/facebookresearch/digit360)
- **[ManiWAV: Learning Robot Manipulation from In-the-Wild Audio-Visual Data](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.19464)** (2024) — An 'ear-in-hand' gripper records audio with vision during human demos in the wild; contact sound then helps policies when vision alone is ambiguous. · [code](https://github.com/real-stanford/maniwav)
- **[Octopi: Object Property Reasoning with Large Tactile-Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.02794)** (2024) — Combines tactile perception with language so an embodied system can reason about physical properties learned by touch; contributes the PhysiCLeAR dataset. · [code](https://github.com/clear-nus/octopi)
- **[RoboPack: Learning Tactile-Informed Dynamics Models for Dense Packing](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.01418)** (2024) — A recurrent graph network estimates object state and latent physics from visuo-tactile history; the learned dynamics model drives MPC for tasks like dense packing.
- **[Sparsh: Self-supervised touch representations for vision-based tactile sensing](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.24090)** (2024) — Self-supervised encoders pretrained on many tactile images that work across different vision-based touch sensors. · [code](https://github.com/facebookresearch/sparsh)
- **[TacSL: A Library for Visuotactile Sensor Simulation and Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.06506)** (2024) — GPU-based simulation of GelSight-style tactile images and contact forces in Isaac Gym, plus learning tools for sim-to-real transfer of visuotactile policies.
- **[Tactile-Augmented Radiance Fields](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.04534)** (2024) — TaRF puts vision and touch in one 3D space: touch probes are registered to photos, and a conditional diffusion model estimates touch at unprobed positions. · [code](https://github.com/dou-yiming/TaRF)
- **[9DTact: A Compact Vision-Based Tactile Sensor for Accurate 3D Shape Reconstruction and Generalizable 6D Force Estimation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2308.14277)** (2023) — Successor to DTact - a compact open-source visuotactile sensor that reconstructs 3D contact shape and estimates 6D force/torque with a model that generalizes to unseen objects. · [code](https://github.com/linchangyi1/9DTact)
- **[Evetac: An Event-based Optical Tactile Sensor for Robotic Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.01236)** (2023) — Replaces the RGB camera of an optical tactile sensor with an event camera; touch processing runs online at 1000 Hz and senses vibrations up to 498 Hz.
- **[General In-Hand Object Rotation with Vision and Touch](https://arxiv.org/abs/2309.09979)** (2023) — RotateIt trains in simulation with privileged object information, then distills to a policy that rotates diverse objects in hand using vision, touch and proprioception on a real hand.
- **[MimicTouch: Leveraging Multi-modal Human Tactile Demonstrations for Contact-rich Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.16917)** (2023) — Learns tactile policies from demonstrations given with human hands, closing the gap of teleop where operators steer by sight while the policy needs touch.
- **[Neural feels with neural fields: Visuo-tactile perception for in-hand manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.13469)** (2023) — NeuralFeels fuses vision and touch from a multi-fingered hand into an online neural field, reconstructing an object's shape and tracking its pose while the hand manipulates it. · [code](https://github.com/facebookresearch/neuralfeels)
- **[Robot Synesthesia: In-Hand Manipulation with Visuotactile Sensing](https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.01853)** (2023) — Turns tactile readings into points in the same 3D frame as the camera point cloud and the hand model, so one visuotactile point-cloud input drives in-hand object rotation. · [code](https://github.com/YingYuan0414/in-hand-rotation) · [project](https://yingyuan0414.github.io/visuotactile/)
- **[Rotating without Seeing: Towards In-hand Dexterity through Touch](https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.10880)** (2023) — Covers a robot hand with cheap binary (contact / no contact) touch sensors and trains an RL policy in simulation that rotates unseen objects in hand on a real robot without any vision. · [code](https://github.com/YingYuan0414/in-hand-rotation) · [project](https://touchdexterity.github.io)
- **[DTact: A Vision-Based Tactile Sensor that Measures High-Resolution 3D Geometry Directly from Darkness](https://arxiv.org/abs/2209.13916)** (2022) — A low-cost visuotactile sensor that reads contact depth from how dark each pixel gets as the gel is pressed, giving high-resolution 3D geometry without photometric stereo.
- **[See, Hear, and Feel: Smart Sensory Fusion for Robotic Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2212.03858)** (2022) — Fuses camera, contact microphone and GelSight touch with self-attention in an imitation-learned policy, studied on dense packing and pouring tasks. · [code](https://github.com/JunzheJosephZhu/see_hear_feel)
- **[Tactile Gym 2.0: Sim-to-real Deep Reinforcement Learning for Comparing Low-cost High-Resolution Robot Touch](https://arxiv.org/abs/2207.10763)** (2022) — Open-source tactile RL environments with sim-to-real transfer across three low-cost optical sensors (TacTip, DIGIT, DigiTac), for comparing them on the same tasks. · [code](https://github.com/dexterousrobot/tactile_gym)
- **[Touch and Go: Learning from Human-Collected Vision and Touch](https://arxiv.org/abs/2211.12498)** (2022) — A dataset of paired video and GelSight touch collected by people probing objects in the wild, for cross-modal tactile learning.
- **[GelSlim 3.0: High-Resolution Measurement of Shape, Force and Slip in a Compact Tactile-Sensing Finger](https://arxiv.org/abs/2103.12269)** (2021) — A slim GelSight-style finger that measures contact shape, force and slip at high resolution, designed to fit robot grippers.
- **[ReSkin: versatile, replaceable, lasting tactile skins](https://arxiv.org/abs/2111.00071)** (2021) — A thin magnetic-elastomer skin read by magnetometers; cheap to make and swap, with learned calibration that carries over to new skins. · [code](https://github.com/raunaqbhirangi/reskin_sensor)
- **[Taxim: An Example-based Simulation Model for GelSight Tactile Sensors](https://arxiv.org/abs/2109.04027)** (2021) — Simulates GelSight images from calibration examples (optical lookup + linear elastic deformation) instead of physics-based rendering, so tactile data can be generated quickly. · [code](https://github.com/Robo-Touch/Taxim)
- **[TACTO: A Fast, Flexible, and Open-source Simulator for High-Resolution Vision-based Tactile Sensors](https://arxiv.org/abs/2012.08456)** (2020) — Renders DIGIT/GelSight-style tactile images in simulation so touch-based policies can be trained at scale. · [code](https://github.com/facebookresearch/tacto)
- **[Making Sense of Vision and Touch: Self-Supervised Learning of Multimodal Representations for Contact-Rich Tasks](https://arxiv.org/abs/1810.10191)** (2018) — Learns a compact vision + force/torque representation with self-supervised objectives, then trains a peg-insertion policy with RL on top of it on a real robot. · [code](https://github.com/stanford-iprl-lab/multimodal_representation)

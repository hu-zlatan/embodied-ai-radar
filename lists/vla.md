- **[DeeR-VLA: Dynamic Inference of Multimodal Large Language Models for Efficient Robot Execution](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.02359)** (2024) — Multi-exit VLA that stops at the smallest model fit for the moment, with budgets for power, latency and GPU memory; on CALVIN it reports 5.2–6.5× lower LLM compute and 2–6× less memory without hurting performance. · [code](https://github.com/yueyang130/DeeR-VLA)
- **[Emma-X: An Embodied Multimodal Action Model with Grounded Chain of Thought and Look-ahead Spatial Reasoning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.11974)** (2024) — A VLA with grounded chain of thought and look-ahead spatial reasoning, trained on a dataset built from BridgeV2 with 60,000 auto-annotated trajectories. · [code](https://github.com/declare-lab/Emma-X)
- **[GR-2: A Generative Video-Language-Action Model with Web-Scale Knowledge for Robot Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.06158)** (2024) — Pretrained to generate future video on 38M clips, then fine-tuned to predict frames and actions together; reports 97.7% average success over 100+ real tabletop tasks and roughly double GR-1's success in unseen scenes. · [project](https://gr2-manipulation.github.io)
- **[Latent Action Pretraining from Videos](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.11758)** (2024) — LAPA pretrains a VLA without action labels: it learns discrete latent actions between video frames, predicts them from image + text, then maps them to robot actions; reports beating OpenVLA on real tasks. · [code](https://github.com/LatentActionPretraining/LAPA) · [project](https://latentactionpretraining.github.io/)
- **[Octo: An Open-Source Generalist Robot Policy](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.12213)** (2024) — A small open diffusion-head Transformer pretrained on Open X-Embodiment that fine-tunes quickly to new robots and sensors. · [code](https://github.com/octo-models/octo)
- **[RDT-1B: a Diffusion Foundation Model for Bimanual Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.07864)** (2024) — A 1.2B diffusion Transformer with a unified action space across robots, targeted at bimanual manipulation. · [code](https://github.com/thu-ml/RoboticsDiffusionTransformer)
- **[RoboMamba: Efficient Vision-Language-Action Model for Robotic Reasoning and Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.04339)** (2024) — An end-to-end VLA built on Mamba state space models, aiming for both robotic reasoning and cheap fine-tuning and inference. · [project](https://sites.google.com/view/robomamba-web)
- **[Robotic Control via Embodied Chain-of-Thought Reasoning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.08693)** (2024) — Trains VLAs to reason through plans, subtasks, motions and visual features before acting; synthetic reasoning data gives OpenVLA a reported 28% success gain on hard generalization tasks with no extra robot data. · [project](https://embodied-cot.github.io)
- **[Scaling Proprioceptive-Visual Learning with Heterogeneous Pre-trained Transformers](https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.20537)** (2024) — HPT: per-robot stems turn proprioception and vision into a few tokens for one shared transformer trunk, pretrained on 52 datasets (robot, sim, human video); reports 20%+ gains on unseen tasks. · [code](https://github.com/liruiw/HPT) · [project](https://liruiw.github.io/hpt/)
- **[TinyVLA: Towards Fast, Data-Efficient Vision-Language-Action Models for Robotic Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.12514)** (2024) — A compact VLA built from a small multimodal model (up to 1.4B) with LoRA and a diffusion policy head, without large-scale robot pretraining; reports higher real-robot success than OpenVLA and ~20x faster inference. · [code](https://github.com/liyaxuanliyaxuan/TinyVLA) · [project](https://tiny-vla.github.io/)
- **[TraceVLA: Visual Trace Prompting Enhances Spatial-Temporal Awareness for Generalist Robotic Policies](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.10345)** (2024) — Draws the robot's recent motion traces on the input image ('visual trace prompting') and fine-tunes OpenVLA on 150K trajectories; reports ~10% gains on SimplerEnv and 3.5x on real WidowX tasks; 4B variant too. · [code](https://github.com/umd-huang-lab/tracevla) · [project](https://tracevla.github.io/)
- **[Foundation Models in Robotics: Applications, Challenges, and the Future](https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.07843)** (2023) — Survey of how pretrained foundation models are used in robot perception, decision-making and control, and what holds them back — scarce robot data, safety and uncertainty, real-time execution. · [project](https://github.com/robotics-survey/Awesome-Robotics-Foundation-Models)
- **[PaLM-E: An Embodied Multimodal Language Model](https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.03378)** (2023) — Injects continuous sensor embeddings into a 562B language model for embodied reasoning and planning.
- **[A Generalist Agent](https://arxiv.org/abs/2205.06175)** (2022) — Gato — one Transformer with one set of weights plays games, captions images and stacks blocks with a real arm.
- **[RT-1: Robotics Transformer for Real-World Control at Scale](https://arxiv.org/abs/2212.06817)** (2022) — Tokenized actions plus a Transformer trained on 130k real demos showed that robot policies scale with data diversity. · [code](https://github.com/google-research/robotics_transformer)
- **[VIMA: General Robot Manipulation with Multimodal Prompts](https://arxiv.org/abs/2210.03094)** (2022) — Casts many tabletop tasks as interleaved text-and-image prompts to one Transformer policy, with the VIMA-Bench suite for systematic generalization tests. · [code](https://github.com/vimalabs/VIMA)

- ⭐⭐⭐ **[ORB-SLAM3: An Accurate Open-Source Library for Visual, Visual-Inertial and Multi-Map SLAM](https://arxiv.org/abs/2007.11898)** (2020) — The go-to feature-based SLAM library — monocular, stereo and RGB-D, with IMU fusion and multi-map relocalization. · [code](https://github.com/UZ-SLAMLab/ORB_SLAM3)
- ⭐⭐ **[Segment Anything](https://arxiv.org/abs/2304.02643)** (2023) — SAM — a promptable segmentation model (points, boxes, masks) trained on 1B+ masks; a standard building block for object masks in robot perception pipelines. · [code](https://github.com/facebookresearch/segment-anything)
- ⭐ **[FoundationStereo: Zero-Shot Stereo Matching](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.09898)** (2025) — Stereo depth model trained on large synthetic data that reports strong zero-shot accuracy on real scenes — a drop-in depth source for stereo-camera robots. · [code](https://github.com/NVlabs/FoundationStereo)
- ⭐ **[SAM 3: Segment Anything with Concepts](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.16719)** (2025) — Segments and tracks every instance of a concept given a short noun phrase or image exemplar, in images and video; trained with a data engine covering 4M concept labels, with open weights and the SA-Co benchmark. · [code](https://github.com/facebookresearch/sam3)
- ⭐ **[VGGT: Visual Geometry Grounded Transformer](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.11651)** (2025) — One feed-forward transformer predicts cameras, depth, point maps and tracks for many views at once, in about a second. · [code](https://github.com/facebookresearch/vggt)
- ⭐ **[Depth Anything V2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.09414)** (2024) — Trains a large teacher only on synthetic images with precise depth, then pseudo-labels many real images to train smaller students; gives sharper, more robust monocular depth than V1. · [code](https://github.com/DepthAnything/Depth-Anything-V2)
- ⭐ **[Depth Anything: Unleashing the Power of Large-Scale Unlabeled Data](https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.10891)** (2024) — Monocular depth model trained on tens of millions of pseudo-labelled images; a common off-the-shelf depth prior for robots. · [code](https://github.com/LiheYoung/Depth-Anything)
- ⭐ **[FAST-LIVO2: Fast, Direct LiDAR-Inertial-Visual Odometry](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.14035)** (2024) — FAST-LIVO2 — fuses LiDAR, camera and IMU in one filter with direct (feature-free) updates for both sensors, sharing a single voxel map where image patches are attached to LiDAR points. · [code](https://github.com/hku-mars/FAST-LIVO2)
- ⭐ **[Grounding Image Matching in 3D with MASt3R](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.09756)** (2024) — Extends DUSt3R with a dense local-feature head, so one network gives both 3D pointmaps and accurate pixel matches between views — useful for calibration-free reconstruction and localization. · [code](https://github.com/naver/mast3r)
- ⭐ **[SAM 2: Segment Anything in Images and Videos](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.00714)** (2024) — Extends SAM to video with a streaming memory, so a mask prompted once is tracked across frames — handy for following objects during manipulation. · [code](https://github.com/facebookresearch/sam2)
- ⭐ **[BundleSDF: Neural 6-DoF Tracking and 3D Reconstruction of Unknown Objects](https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.14158)** (2023) — Tracks the 6-DoF pose of a never-seen object in RGB-D video while building its neural 3D model online, with no CAD model needed; useful for manipulating novel objects. · [code](https://github.com/NVlabs/BundleSDF)
- ⭐ **[CoTracker: It is Better to Track Together](https://arxiv.org/abs/2307.07635)** (2023) — Transformer that tracks many video points jointly rather than one at a time, staying robust through occlusions; widely used to extract motion tracks from human or robot videos for policy learning. · [code](https://github.com/facebookresearch/co-tracker)
- ⭐ **[DINOv2: Learning Robust Visual Features without Supervision](https://arxiv.org/abs/2304.07193)** (2023) — Self-supervised image features that work frozen for depth, segmentation and retrieval; a common visual backbone in VLAs (e.g. OpenVLA) and latent world models. · [code](https://github.com/facebookresearch/dinov2)
- ⭐ **[DUSt3R: Geometric 3D Vision Made Easy](https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.14132)** (2023) — Regresses 3D point maps straight from image pairs without known camera intrinsics or poses; started the "3D foundation model" wave. · [code](https://github.com/naver/dust3r)
- ⭐ **[FoundationPose: Unified 6D Pose Estimation and Tracking of Novel Objects](https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.08344)** (2023) — 6D pose estimation and tracking of unseen objects from a CAD model or a few reference images, with no per-object training. · [code](https://github.com/NVlabs/FoundationPose)
- ⭐ **[Gaussian Splatting SLAM](https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.06741)** (2023) — MonoGS — runs SLAM directly on a 3D Gaussian map, giving camera tracking and a photorealistic dense map from one camera. · [code](https://github.com/muskie82/MonoGS)
- ⭐ **[Grounding DINO: Marrying DINO with Grounded Pre-Training for Open-Set Object Detection](https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.05499)** (2023) — Open-set detector that finds objects from a free-text prompt; often paired with SAM ("Grounded-SAM") to turn a language instruction into object masks. · [code](https://github.com/IDEA-Research/GroundingDINO)
- ⭐ **[DROID-SLAM: Deep Visual SLAM for Monocular, Stereo, and RGB-D Cameras](https://arxiv.org/abs/2108.10869)** (2021) — End-to-end learned visual SLAM — recurrent optical-flow updates plus a differentiable bundle-adjustment layer. · [code](https://github.com/princeton-vl/DROID-SLAM)
- ⭐ **[FAST-LIO2: Fast Direct LiDAR-inertial Odometry](https://arxiv.org/abs/2107.06829)** (2021) — Registers raw LiDAR points directly to the map with an incremental k-d tree; fast enough for onboard drone and robot computers. · [code](https://github.com/hku-mars/FAST_LIO)
- ⭐ **[LIO-SAM: Tightly-coupled Lidar Inertial Odometry via Smoothing and Mapping](https://arxiv.org/abs/2007.00258)** (2020) — LiDAR-inertial odometry built on a factor graph, so GPS and loop closures slot in as extra factors. · [code](https://github.com/TixiaoShan/LIO-SAM)
- ⭐ **[Kimera: an Open-Source Library for Real-Time Metric-Semantic Localization and Mapping](https://arxiv.org/abs/1910.02490)** (2019) — Open C++ library for real-time metric-semantic visual-inertial SLAM: VIO, robust pose graph, fast 3D mesher and dense semantic reconstruction as modular parts. · [code](https://github.com/MIT-SPARK/Kimera)
- **[ACE-Brain-0.6: Scaling Spatial Grounding for Physical Foundation Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.27040)** (2026) — Fine-tunes Qwen3.5-9B on ~1.5M grounding samples auto-built from robot recordings plus other visual data; reports a 75.41 average over seven grounding benchmarks vs 73.68 for GPT-6 Astra. · [code](https://github.com/DAXIAORobotics/ACE-Brain-0.6) · [project](https://daxiaorobotics.github.io/ACE-Brain-0.6/)
- **[AURORA: Active Uncertainty-Driven Re-Orientation for In-Hand Reconstruction](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.08493)** (2026) — Active in-hand 3D reconstruction: estimates per-ray reconstruction uncertainty to pick the next view, then an in-hand rotation policy turns the object to expose it. Better quality and efficiency than non-active rotation. · [project](https://aurorahand.github.io/)
- **[Anchor3R: Streaming 3D Reconstruction with Transient Anchors for Long-Horizon Visual Mapping](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.05035)** (2026) — CoRL 2026 Spotlight. Current-centric streaming 3D reconstruction that predicts window-relative poses and local geometry in the current frame, to limit drift on long runs. · [code](https://github.com/polar-explorer/Anchor3R)
- **[AnchorHEC: Targetless Hand-Eye Calibration of Wrist and Fixed Cameras](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.02884)** (2026) — Calibrates wrist and fixed cameras together with no target, from RGB, kinematics and a 3D foundation model; reports 86.4% lower translation and 71.6% lower rotation error in simulation than reconstruction baselines. · [code](https://github.com/HwangGB/AnchorHEC) · [project](https://hwanggb.github.io/AnchorHEC/)
- **[Articulated 3D Scene Graphs for Open-World Mobile Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.16356)** (2026) — MoMa-SG builds 3D scene graphs that encode how objects articulate, from RGB-D sequences, and adds the Arti4D-Semantic dataset; demonstrated on a quadruped and a mobile manipulator. · [code](https://github.com/robot-learning-freiburg/MoMa-SG) · [project](https://momasg.cs.uni-freiburg.de)
- **[CoReMatch: Recurrent Continuous Coordinate Refinement for Real-Time Robotic Feature Matching](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.03932)** (2026) — Feature matcher that refines correspondences recurrently in continuous coordinates instead of on fixed grids; reports higher accuracy than Efficient LoFTR at over 10x its speed on embedded hardware.
- **[Cyclops: LiDAR as a Camera That Dreams in Color](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.16264)** (2026) — Turns sparse LiDAR intensity into RGB-like video so camera models work in the dark. Reports better segmentation and lane detection than LiDAR baselines and real cameras across lighting. · [code](https://github.com/IMRL/Cyclops)
- **[Fast and Accurate Hand Estimation in Pixels, Interaction and World Space](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.07711)** (2026) — FAH builds on SAM 3D Body's hand branch: predict 2D/3D joints and surface points, then fit MANO or MHR-Hand, with optional contact and world-space tracking. The default ViT-L runs at 65 fps on an RTX 4070 Ti SUPER. · [code](https://github.com/noahcao/FAH)
- **[FreeLoc: Online Floorplan Localization via Diffusion-Aided Pose Refinement](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.05011)** (2026) — Localizes an RGB camera on a floorplan online, querying the map directly and refining poses with diffusion instead of building an offline database. Reports SOTA single-frame and sequential results in real time. · [project](https://zju3dv.github.io/freeloc/)
- **[Functional-SLAM: Interaction-Aware Mapping with Online Functional Scene Graphs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.07497)** (2026) — SLAM that keeps a functional scene graph (which handle opens which drawer) as online state, and uses that topology to help loop closure in repetitive scenes. Reported faster than offline methods at similar accuracy. · [code](https://github.com/Hbelief1998/Functional-SLAM-CoRL_2026)
- **[G2G: Exploiting Intra-Group Geometry for Inter-Group Pose Estimation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.08284)** (2026) — Relative 6-DoF pose between two image groups (relocalization, multi-camera rigs): keeps a multi-view foundation model frozen and adds ~32M trainable parameters (resampler, cross-group bridge, pose head). · [code](https://github.com/WeiYuFei0217/G2G)
- **[GINIO: A Geometric SO(3)-Equivariant Interface for Neural Inertial Odometry](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.25338)** (2026) — Neural inertial odometry whose mean and covariance transform correctly under any IMU mounting rotation; Last-Frame Alignment makes sensor-frame training match world-frame. TLIO ATE: 2.018 m vs 76.389 m for EqNIO.
- **[HiPFuse: Real-Time LiDAR Semantic Segmentation for Robotic Perception on Edge Devices](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.14025)** (2026) — Point-BEV fusion with height-partitioned BEV features for LiDAR semantics: 63.7% mIoU on SemanticKITTI at 14.2 FPS on a Jetson AGX Orin. Swapping it into a SLAM front end lowers trajectory error by 10.6% on average. · [code](https://github.com/haiyang2022/HiPFuse)
- **[LIME: Learning Intent-aware Camera Motion from Egocentric Video](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.02417)** (2026) — Treats camera motion as an action: given an image and a language intent ('look behind the box'), predict the next relative camera pose. Supervision is mined from egocentric video by hindsight labeling.
- **[Language as a Sensor: Calibrated Spatial Belief Estimation in 3D Scenes from Natural Language](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.08666)** (2026) — Treats spoken descriptions ('my bag is on the table') as sensor readings: a model outputs a calibrated spatial distribution that is fused with onboard perception in one belief map. VLA-3D benchmark + real robot. · [code](https://github.com/MIT-SPARK/Language-As-A-Sensor) · [project](https://aryan-naveen.github.io/language-as-a-sensor/)
- **[LateMatch: Breaking the Interaction Bottleneck via Cacheable Matching for Efficient Visual Localization](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.25358)** (2026) — Restructures deep feature matchers so cross-image interaction happens only in the last layers; per-image context blocks are cached offline. Reports over 2x lower compute, plus 10-14% from entropy-guided token reduction.
- **[Learning Adaptive Solvers for Distributed Factor Graph Optimization on Matrix Lie Groups](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.08735)** (2026) — DeepCORD unrolls a parallel accelerated Riemannian optimizer and learns a self-supervised policy that tunes solver parameters by phase and communication status. Tested on SE(3) pose graphs and SL(4).
- **[MosaicIMU: Composing Carrier Experts for Generalizable Neural Inertial Odometry](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.09355)** (2026) — Neural inertial odometry across carriers: a router composes carrier-specific experts, and a light residual branch adapts to new ones. Reports 40% lower ATE than learned baselines.
- **[OpenVIO: A Versatile Open-Source Visual-Inertial Odometry System](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.41671)** (2026) — MIT-licensed visual-inertial odometry for research and products. Supports pinhole and fisheye cameras plus stitched 360° images, with benchmarks against other open-source VIO systems on public datasets. · [code](https://github.com/93won/openvio)
- **[OptiGeo: Efficient Monocular Geometry for Embodied Perception in Optically Challenging Scenes](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.29881)** (2026) — 30M-parameter monocular depth model for transparent and reflective scenes, trained to undo depth-sensor bias with a small rendered set. Reports beating 300M-scale and multi-view models on transparent benchmarks. · [code](https://github.com/mx-liu6/OptiGeo) · [project](https://mx-liu6.github.io/OptiGeo-web/)
- **[PROSE: Training-Free Egocentric Scene Registration with Vision-Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.16569)** (2026) — Aligns two RGB-only egocentric captures of a room taken at different times: builds object-level 3D scene graphs with foundation models and lets a VLM match objects across them. No training or depth sensor.
- **[ReSiReg: Towards Spatially Consistent Semantics in Language-Conditioned Robotic Tasks](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.19088)** (2026) — Makes dense VLM features spatially consistent by rebuilding each patch from language-labelled visual prototypes; improves open-vocabulary retrieval and 3D mapping. Also ships a compact 25M dense VLM for robots. · [code](https://github.com/SimonSchwaiger/resireg) · [project](https://resireg.github.io)
- **[RoMo-GS: Efficient Online 3D Gaussian Splatting for Robot Mapping via Exposure-Integrated Motion Blur](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.24605)** (2026) — Online 3DGS mapping that integrates each Gaussian over the exposure time analytically to handle motion blur, and prunes by a Hessian score. Matches offline deblurring 3DGS at 15-150x the speed.
- **[SpaRV: Sparse Radiance Voxels for Scalable Geometrically Consistent LiDAR-Visual Mapping and Rendering](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.25665)** (2026) — A sparse-voxel radiance map built from LiDAR and camera data, aimed at robot logs — few viewpoints, imperfect poses and large scenes — while keeping geometry accurate and GPU memory in check. · [code](https://github.com/hku-mars/SpaRV)
- **[Vision as Proprioception: Estimating Humanoid Robot Pose from Video](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.25288)** (2026) — HumanoidPose recovers a humanoid's full-body joint configuration from video alone — useful when onboard state is unavailable, and for turning online robot videos into training data. · [code](https://github.com/eth-siplab/HumanoidPose)
- **[VoxAfford: Multi-Scale Voxel-Token Fusion for Open-Vocabulary 3D Affordance Detection](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.01365)** (2026) — Open-vocabulary 3D affordance detection: MLLM output tokens pull multi-scale geometry from a frozen 3D VQ-VAE through gated cross-attention before mask decoding. Reports about +8% mIoU.
- **[What Objects Enable, Not What They Are: Functional Latent Spaces for Affordance Reasoning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.05533)** (2026) — Maps observations into a latent space organised by affordances (e.g. 'movable') rather than appearance, and adds new affordances when uncertain. Reports 94% inference accuracy, 15+ points above prior work. · [project](https://A4Dance-reasoning.github.io)
- **[Which Depth Estimator for Jetson AGX Orin? A 2026 Benchmark of Monocular and Stereo Methods](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.27199)** (2026) — Measured numbers for robot builders: nine monocular and stereo depth families (Depth Anything V2/3, Depth Pro, MoGe-2, Fast-FoundationStereo, ...) run on a Jetson AGX Orin, scored for metric accuracy and speed. · [code](https://github.com/nodarhub/orin-depth-benchmark)
- **[RoboRefer: Towards Spatial Referring with Reasoning in Vision-Language Models for Robotics](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.04308)** (2025) — RoboRefer trains a VLM for spatial referring: reasoning about which 3D location an instruction points to. · [code](https://github.com/Zhoues/RoboRefer) · [project](https://zhoues.github.io/RoboRefer/)
- **[Towards Spatial Trace with Reasoning in Vision-Language Models for Robotics](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.13660)** (2025) — RoboTracer is a 3D-aware VLM for spatial tracing, which needs multi-step metric reasoning about real-world measurements. · [code](https://github.com/Zhoues/RoboTracer) · [project](https://zhoues.github.io/RoboTracer)

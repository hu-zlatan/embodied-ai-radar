- ⭐ **[Precise and Dexterous Robotic Manipulation via Human-in-the-Loop Reinforcement Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.21845)** (2024) — HIL-SERL — real-world RL with human corrections, reported to reach near-perfect success on precise assembly and dynamic tasks after a few hours of training. · [code](https://github.com/rail-berkeley/hil-serl)
- ⭐ **[Proximal Policy Optimization Algorithms](https://arxiv.org/abs/1707.06347)** (2017) — The clipped policy-gradient method behind most GPU-parallel sim-to-real locomotion and dexterity training.
- **[ACCRUE: Continual Off-Policy Reinforcement Learning for Robot Foundation Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.18596)** (2026) — Continual off-policy RL for VLAs — small actor/critic networks carry over between tasks, distilled on a few kept episodes to avoid forgetting; reports 31–68.5% less real-robot time on new assembly tasks. · [project](https://accrue-rl.github.io)
- **[AgenticRoboRL: Bringing Coding-Agent Post-Training to Agentic Robotics](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.28589)** (2026) — Post-trains an open 9B robot planner (SFT + multi-turn RL) across harnesses that call VLA skills or write control code; reports LIBERO-PRO success rising from 18.1% to 71.0% without external memory. · [code](https://github.com/xinxinliu-ai/AgenticRoboRL) · [project](https://xinxinliu-ai.github.io/AgenticRoboRL/)
- **[Behavior Graphs: Unsupervised Graph Structures for Interpreting Robot Deployment Data](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.00405)** (2026) — Compresses policy rollouts into one directed graph of behavior modes linked to outcomes, to guide what data to collect next. In a user study on three real tasks, 16 of 18 participants preferred it to rollout videos. · [project](https://try.behavior-graphs.com/)
- **[Beyond Validation Loss: Coverage and Reliability Dynamics in Robot Policy Pretraining](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.01565)** (2026) — Tracks closed-loop pass@k, the chance that one of k rollouts succeeds. Checkpoints with equal pass@1 can post-train differently, so it proposes offline checkpoint selection that balances coverage and reliability. · [project](https://zhanyisun.github.io/robot-policy-learning-dynamics/)
- **[Control-Ready Uncertainty for Trajectory Diffusion](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.12431)** (2026) — SCOPE gives diffusion trajectory models calibrated uncertainty tubes without Monte Carlo sampling, by distilling score curvature; tested in crowd navigation and Franka manipulation. · [project](https://zackaxue.github.io/SCOPE-project-page/)
- **[CuraEval: Evaluating Demonstration Curation on Mixed-Skill Robot Data](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.38221)** (2026) — Sanity check for demo-curation methods on mixed-skill robomimic data: one score ranks demos almost exactly like episode length, and after Holm correction no curated subset significantly beats training on all data. · [code](https://github.com/jiuyixu25/CuraEval)
- **[D&A: Decoupling Description and Assessment Enables VLMs as Zero-Shot Robotic Reward Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.03622)** (2026) — Prompt-only task-progress scoring: the VLM first describes what happens and defines absolute success, then scores with an explicit rubric. This curbs hallucinated progress on failed runs, and the rewards can drive RL. · [code](https://github.com/memmelma/dna) · [project](https://weirdlabuw.github.io/dna)
- **[Diffusion ReRoll: Revisable Denoising for Robotic Sequential Prediction](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.19919)** (2026) — CoRL 2026 Spotlight. Diffusion sequence prediction with selective re-noising, so segments of the horizon can be refined again using context from the rest. · [project](https://seonsoo-p1.github.io/DiffusionReRoll/)
- **[DiscoDemo: Discovering Efficient and Diverse Robot Demonstrations for Imitation Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.32093)** (2026) — Uses RL as a demo generator, not a controller: a latent-conditioned policy finds several distinct successful solutions, exported as an imitation dataset; reports 78% real-robot success vs. 39% for the best baseline. · [code](https://github.com/DAVIAN-Robotics/DiscoDemo) · [project](https://davian-robotics.github.io/DiscoDemo)
- **[ELA: End-to-End Latent Action Policy Learning from Video Demonstrations](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.05571)** (2026) — Jointly trains a latent action model, latent policy and action decoder, so plentiful action-free video plus a few labelled robot demos can train a policy. Reports beating prior latent-action baselines. · [project](https://end2end-latent-action.github.io/)
- **[EgoForesight: Effectively Scaling Policy Learning with Noisy Egocentric Actions](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31845)** (2026) — Co-trains a VLA on robot demos and noisy egocentric-video actions by weighting each source by reliability and distilling from clean future frames; reports steeper scaling than the baseline up to 30k hours of data. · [code](https://github.com/LinShan-Bin/EgoForesight) · [project](https://lin-shan.com/EgoForesight)
- **[FedGuide: Diffusion Prior Alignment and Value Baseline Guidance for Heterogeneous Federated Reinforcement Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.18964)** (2026) — Federated RL for heterogeneous clients that aggregates diffusion behavior priors with optimal transport rather than averaging policies, plus a low-variance DICE value baseline.
- **[Flexible Language-Guided Representation Editing for Adaptive Robot Behavior](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.28893)** (2026) — FLEX lets language corrections edit a robot's task features via add, generalize and mask (not add-only). Reports higher normalized reward than other editing methods in 2D nav, Franka tabletop and a real user study. · [project](https://mit-clear-lab.github.io/FLEX/)
- **[Flow Policy Gradients for Robot Control](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.02481)** (2026) — CoRL 2026 Oral. Makes flow matching policy gradients work for robot control, bypassing action likelihoods, so policies need not be Gaussian. Applied to legged locomotion, humanoid motion tracking and manipulation. · [code](https://github.com/amazon-far/fpo-control) · [project](https://hongsukchoi.github.io/fpo-control)
- **[FlowDAgger: Human-in-the-Loop Adaptation of Generative Robot Policies in Latent Space](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.08877)** (2026) — CoRL 2026 Spotlight. Adapts frozen generative policies from human interventions in latent space, as a cheaper alternative to mass data collection or online RL on hardware. · [code](https://github.com/microsoft/FlowDAgger) · [project](https://microsoft.github.io/FlowDAgger)
- **[GSBR: Geometric Support Behavior Regularization for Online RL](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.05295)** (2026) — Online RL on top of a pretrained policy, penalised by distance from that policy's support: free inside it, smoothly costly outside; reports average success rising from 33% to 95% across 19 benchmark tasks. · [project](https://charlesxu0124.github.io/gsbr/)
- **[HIDRA: Hierarchical Dual-Routing Attention for Replay-Free Lifelong Imitation Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.36374)** (2026) — Replay-free lifelong imitation learning: hierarchical dual-routing attention retrieves experts by instruction, then refines by context, plus key regularization against drift. Reports beating replay baselines on LIBERO. · [code](https://github.com/yfqi/HIDRA)
- **[LLMs as MDP Designers: A Dependency-Aware Agentic Framework for Automated Robot Policy Generation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.04974)** (2026) — ARPG has an LLM design the RL pipeline before deployment: MDP first, then simulator code, with checks that state, action and reward agree; reports more successful policies than monolithic LLM baselines in Isaac Sim.
- **[Learning from Fleet Experience: Collective Experience Learning Across 96 Robots](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.25736)** (2026) — Deployed robots pool their runs; failures retrieve matching successful segments from the fleet buffer to fine-tune on, with no new demos. Reports 93–99% on five real tasks; 96 robots lift phone packing 79% → 98%. · [code](https://github.com/RLinf/RLinf) · [project](https://cel-robot-learning.github.io)
- **[Progress-Guided Preference Generation for Aligning Generative Control Policies](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.25692)** (2026) — Fine-tunes diffusion and flow policies with automatic preference labels: rollouts are ranked by progress measured against a few expert demos, no human labels or reward design. Reports beating SFT on long-horizon tasks. · [project](https://pgpg2026.github.io/)
- **[RACE: Calibrated Cumulative Scoring for Rollout-Free Robot Policy Checkpoint Selection](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.05838)** (2026) — Ranks checkpoints without rollouts: action deviations are calibrated against state-dependent thresholds from held-out demos, then combined in a running product. Reports rankings that align with real deployment. · [project](https://changyeon.site/race)
- **[RL Token: Bootstrapping Online RL with Vision-Language-Action Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.23073)** (2026) — CoRL 2026 Oral. Adapts a VLA to expose a compact RL token, an interface for sample-efficient online RL, so a pretrained policy gains precision and speed from a few hours of real-world practice.
- **[Recursive Self-Improvement in Physical AI: Mechanisms, Evidence, and Evaluation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.38266)** (2026) — Survey separating ordinary learner updates from improvers that revise how a robot learns, with an L0–L3 rubric from task-local adaptation to verified improver gain, and proposed comparisons from shared learner starts. · [project](https://github.com/nkd-lkz/physical-ai-rsi-survey)
- **[SCoCaT: Success Conditioned Constrained Reinforcement Learning for Spacecraft Docking](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.06061)** (2026) — Shows termination-based constrained RL can learn to avoid the goal when constraints tighten near it, and fixes this with a dense success critic; validated on spacecraft docking tasks.
- **[Scaling in Games: Continued Pre-Training For Embodied Agents In Diverse Virtual Worlds](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.28698)** (2026) — Continued pre-training study for game-playing agents: 120 runs over 0.8B–27B models, 50–1,600 h of data and multi-world mixes; reports diversity aids transfer to related worlds, little to distant ones. · [code](https://github.com/THUSI-Lab/GameScaling) · [project](https://gamescaling.github.io)
- **[TARC: Time-Adaptive Robotic Control](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.23176)** (2026) — A policy predicts each action and how long to hold it. Reports matching high-frequency control at under half the rate on an RC car and Unitree Go1, plus simulated VLA inference. · [project](https://arnavsukhija.github.io/projects/tarc)
- **[TILDE: Teacher-in-the-Loop Distillation from Embodied Foundation Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.25376)** (2026) — Distills a pretrained VLA teacher into a policy without action pretraining via on-policy supervision plus teacher-run recoveries, triggered by a reward model (AutoDAgger); reports up to 4.1x over BC on 3 real tasks. · [code](https://github.com/hku-sail/TILDE) · [project](https://hku-sail.github.io/TILDE/)
- **[Toward Recursive Self-Improvement in Physical AI](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.29677)** (2026) — Survey of recursive self-improvement in robot learning — improving skills, then the process that makes them — and when gains carry forward; notes no physical study has yet isolated gains from recursion itself. · [project](https://github.com/Physical2RSI/Awesome-Physical2RSI)
- **[Training in Deployment: Human-in-the-loop Online Post-Training for Robot Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.29264)** (2026) — TIDE updates a pretrained policy online from human interventions and its own rollouts, with no critic or reward model; reports gains on four real bimanual tasks, each session finishing in under an hour. · [project](https://logosroboticsgroup.github.io/TIDE/)
- **[When Robots Outweigh Objects: How Inertia Mismatch Shapes Robot Policy Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.03501)** (2026) — Simulation study varying robot and object mass: RL copes, but behavior cloning does better when the robot is lighter than the object, and more data narrows the gap without closing it. Lower-inertia demos help. · [project](https://younghyopark.me/inertia-mismatch/)
- **[Where is Physical RSI capability from? An Observational Scaling Law of LLM-as-policy](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.26848)** (2026) — PhysRSI-eval scores VLM agents on 18 manipulation tasks under a fixed trial budget on five axes, incl. learning from failure; reports similar benchmark averages hide different improvement speeds. · [code](https://github.com/PhysRSI-Eval/PhysRSI-Eval-code) · [project](https://physrsi-eval.github.io/)
- **[Flow Q-Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.02538)** (2025) — FQL — a flow-matching BC policy plus a one-step policy that maximizes Q while distilling from it; no backprop through flow steps, strong on 73 OGBench / D4RL offline and offline-to-online tasks. · [code](https://github.com/seohongpark/fql) · [project](https://seohong.me/projects/fql/)
- **[RLinf-VLA: A Unified and Efficient Framework for Reinforcement Learning of Vision-Language-Action Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.06710)** (2025) — Open RL training framework for VLAs (PPO/GRPO across GPU-parallel simulators); reports 98.11% on 130 LIBERO tasks and 97.66% on 25 ManiSkill tasks after RL — a common base for VLA+RL work. · [code](https://github.com/RLinf/RLinf)
- **[SimpleVLA-RL: Scaling VLA Training via Reinforcement Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.09674)** (2025) — Online RL for VLAs with GRPO and only binary success rewards on OpenVLA-OFT, plus exploration tweaks; reports 99.1% on LIBERO and 70.4% on RoboTwin 1.0 (vs 39.8% for the SFT baseline). · [code](https://github.com/PRIME-RL/SimpleVLA-RL)
- **[Steering Your Diffusion Policy with Latent Space Reinforcement Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.15799)** (2025) — DSRL — adapts a frozen diffusion policy by running RL over its input noise, not its weights; needs only forward passes, works with π0, and reports real-robot success rising from 20% to 90% in under 50 online episodes. · [code](https://github.com/ajwagen/dsrl) · [project](https://diffusion-steering.github.io)
- **[Towards Embodiment Scaling Laws in Robot Locomotion](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.05753)** (2025) — Tests 'embodiment scaling laws' on about 1,000 generated robots: does training on more bodies improve generalization to unseen ones? · [project](https://embodiment-scaling-laws.github.io/)
- **[Diffusion Policy Policy Optimization](https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.00588)** (2024) — DPPO — fine-tunes a pre-trained diffusion policy with PPO by treating the denoising steps as part of the MDP; reports stable gains over other RL fine-tuning methods and sim-to-real transfer. · [code](https://github.com/irom-princeton/dppo)
- **[SERL: A Software Suite for Sample-Efficient Robotic Reinforcement Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.16013)** (2024) — An open-source stack for training manipulation policies with RL on a real arm — learned reward classifiers, reset-free training and an impedance controller. · [code](https://github.com/rail-berkeley/serl)
- **[Cal-QL: Calibrated Offline RL Pre-Training for Efficient Online Fine-Tuning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.05479)** (2023) — Keeps conservative offline Q-values from dropping below a reference policy's value, which avoids the early performance dip when an offline-pretrained policy starts online fine-tuning. · [code](https://github.com/nakamotoo/Cal-QL)
- **[Efficient Online Reinforcement Learning with Offline Data](https://arxiv.org/abs/2302.02948)** (2023) — RLPD — a few simple changes to off-policy RL (symmetric sampling of offline and online data, layer norm, ensembles) make prior data speed up online learning. · [code](https://github.com/ikostrikov/rlpd)
- **[Q-Transformer: Scalable Offline Reinforcement Learning via Autoregressive Q-Functions](https://arxiv.org/abs/2309.10150)** (2023) — Discretizes each action dimension and predicts Q-values per dimension autoregressively with a Transformer, plus a conservative regularizer; trained offline on large real robot datasets mixing demos and autonomous data.
- **[Behavior Transformers: Cloning $k$ modes with one stone](https://arxiv.org/abs/2206.11251)** (2022) — BeT — clusters actions into k bins plus a continuous offset so a transformer can imitate multimodal demonstrations instead of averaging them. · [code](https://github.com/notmahi/bet)
- **[Decision Transformer: Reinforcement Learning via Sequence Modeling](https://arxiv.org/abs/2106.01345)** (2021) — Recasts RL as sequence modeling — a GPT-style model predicts actions conditioned on the desired return; an early bridge between transformers and control. · [code](https://github.com/kzl/decision-transformer)
- **[Implicit Behavioral Cloning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2109.00137)** (2021) — Represents the policy as an energy-based model over actions instead of a regressor, which handles multimodal and discontinuous demonstrations much better.
- **[Mastering Visual Continuous Control: Improved Data-Augmented Reinforcement Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2107.09645)** (2021) — DrQ-v2 - a simple model-free actor-critic with image augmentation that learns continuous control from pixels (incl. humanoid tasks in the DeepMind Control Suite) on a single GPU; a common visual-RL baseline. · [code](https://github.com/facebookresearch/drqv2)
- **[Offline Reinforcement Learning with Implicit Q-Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2110.06169)** (2021) — IQL — learns values with expectile regression without ever querying actions outside the dataset, then extracts a policy by advantage-weighted regression; a common base for offline-to-online robot RL. · [code](https://github.com/ikostrikov/implicit_q_learning)
- **[What Matters in Learning from Offline Human Demonstrations for Robot Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2108.03298)** (2021) — robomimic — a controlled study of imitation and offline RL on human-collected manipulation demos (demo quality, observation choices, history-aware BC), released with the framework and datasets. · [code](https://github.com/ARISE-Initiative/robomimic)
- **[AWAC: Accelerating Online Reinforcement Learning with Offline Datasets](https://arxiv.org/abs/2006.09359)** (2020) — Advantage-weighted actor-critic that pre-trains on prior demonstrations and keeps improving online, shown on real dexterous-hand tasks.
- **[Conservative Q-Learning for Offline Reinforcement Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2006.04779)** (2020) — CQL — penalizes Q-values on actions outside the dataset so the learned value is a lower bound; a standard offline-RL baseline for learning from fixed robot logs. · [code](https://github.com/aviralkumar2907/CQL)
- **[Addressing Function Approximation Error in Actor-Critic Methods](https://arxiv.org/abs/1802.09477)** (2018) — TD3 — fixes DDPG's value overestimation with twin critics, delayed policy updates and target smoothing; still a standard continuous-control baseline. · [code](https://github.com/sfujim/TD3)
- **[QT-Opt: Scalable Deep Reinforcement Learning for Vision-Based Robotic Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/1806.10293)** (2018) — Off-policy Q-learning with a cross-entropy-method action optimizer, trained on several hundred thousand real grasp attempts collected by a fleet of robots; reports 96% grasp success on unseen objects.
- **[Soft Actor-Critic: Off-Policy Maximum Entropy Deep Reinforcement Learning with a Stochastic Actor](https://arxiv.org/abs/1801.01290)** (2018) — Off-policy RL with an entropy bonus; sample-efficient enough to become the default for learning directly on real robots.
- **[Generative Adversarial Imitation Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/1606.03476)** (2016) — GAIL — learns a policy whose state-action distribution a discriminator cannot tell apart from the expert's, skipping explicit reward recovery.
- **[A Reduction of Imitation Learning and Structured Prediction to No-Regret Online Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/1011.0686)** (2010) — DAgger — query the expert on the states the learner itself visits, fixing the compounding errors of plain behavior cloning.

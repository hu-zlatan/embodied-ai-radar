- **[ExBody2: Advanced Expressive Humanoid Whole-Body Control](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.13196)** (2024) — Expressive whole-body tracking trained on mocap and simulated data, decoupling whole-body velocity tracking from body-landmark tracking. · [project](https://exbody2.github.io)
- **[Expressive Whole-Body Control for Humanoid Robots](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.16796)** (2024) — Imitates upper-body human motion capture while keeping robust legs, giving humanoids expressive gestures and dance.
- **[HOVER: Versatile Neural Whole-Body Controller for Humanoid Robots](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.21229)** (2024) — Distills one policy that supports several humanoid control modes (root velocity, joint angles, keypoint tracking) instead of training a separate controller per mode.
- **[Harmon: Whole-Body Motion Generation of Humanoid Robots from Language Descriptions](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.12773)** (2024) — Generates humanoid whole-body motion from language, starting from human motion priors and refining with LLM commonsense reasoning. Oral at CoRL 2024. · [project](https://ut-austin-rpl.github.io/Harmon/)
- **[HumanPlus: Humanoid Shadowing and Imitation from Humans](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.10454)** (2024) — A humanoid shadows a human from one RGB camera, then learns autonomous skills from the collected data. · [code](https://github.com/MarkFzp/humanplus)
- **[Humanoid Parkour Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.10759)** (2024) — Learns a vision-based whole-body parkour policy for the Unitree H1 without motion references, covering skills such as jumping onto platforms and leaping over gaps, and transfers it to the real robot.
- **[Learning Human-to-Humanoid Real-Time Whole-Body Teleoperation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.04436)** (2024) — H2O — an RL tracking policy lets an RGB camera stream a person's whole-body motion onto a humanoid in real time.
- **[Learning Smooth Humanoid Locomotion through Lipschitz-Constrained Policies](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.11825)** (2024) — Imposes a Lipschitz constraint on the learned policy as a general route to smooth motion, replacing hand-tuned low-pass filters and smoothness rewards. · [code](https://github.com/zixuan417/smooth-humanoid-locomotion)
- **[Mobile-TeleVision: Predictive Motion Priors for Humanoid Whole-Body Control](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.07773)** (2024) — Keeps IK and retargeting for precise upper-body control, and uses RL with Predictive Motion Priors for robust legs.
- **[OmniH2O: Universal and Dexterous Human-to-Humanoid Whole-Body Teleoperation and Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.08858)** (2024) — One RL whole-body controller that a humanoid can follow from a VR headset, verbal commands or an RGB camera, and that also runs learned autonomous policies.
- **[One Policy to Run Them All: an End-to-end Learning Approach to Multi-Embodiment Locomotion](https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.06366)** (2024) — URMA: one end-to-end architecture that controls many legged morphologies, including quadrupeds, humanoids and hexapods. · [code](https://github.com/nico-bohlinger/one_policy_to_run_them_all)
- **[WoCoCo: Learning Whole-Body Humanoid Control with Sequential Contacts](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.06005)** (2024) — Learns humanoid tasks as sequences of contacts, reducing the task-specific tuning and state machines RL usually needs. · [code](https://github.com/LeCAR-Lab/wococo) · [project](https://lecar-lab.github.io/wococo/)
- **[ANYmal Parkour: Learning Agile Navigation for Quadrupedal Robots](https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.14874)** (2023) — A learned navigation policy sequences pre-trained skills (walk, jump, climb, crouch) using a learned obstacle reconstruction; reports up to 2 m/s on hardware (Science Robotics).
- **[Deep Imitation Learning for Humanoid Loco-manipulation through Human Teleoperation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2309.01952)** (2023) — TRILL collects VR demonstrations and uses a whole-body controller to turn task-space commands into joint torques, for data-efficient loco-manipulation imitation. · [code](https://github.com/UT-Austin-RPL/TRILL) · [project](https://ut-austin-rpl.github.io/TRILL)
- **[DreamWaQ: Learning Robust Quadrupedal Locomotion With Implicit Terrain Imagination via Deep Reinforcement Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2301.10602)** (2023) — A blind quadruped policy that infers terrain properties implicitly from proprioception history with a context-aided estimator, enabling robust walking on stairs and rough ground without cameras.
- **[Prompt a Robot to Walk with Large Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2309.09969)** (2023) — Directly prompts an LLM to output a robot's walking actions, an early test of grounding LLMs in low-level control. · [code](https://github.com/HybridRobotics/prompt2walk) · [project](https://prompt2walk.github.io/)
- **[Learning robust perceptive locomotion for quadrupedal robots in the wild](https://arxiv.org/abs/2201.08117)** (2022) — ANYmal fuses proprioception with noisy height-map perception through a learned belief encoder, and is shown hiking an alpine trail and other rough terrain.
- **[AMP: Adversarial Motion Priors for Stylized Physics-Based Character Control](https://arxiv.org/abs/2104.02180)** (2021) — A discriminator trained on unstructured mocap clips acts as a style reward, so RL learns tasks with natural motion without hand-picked reference clips; widely reused as a gait prior for legged robots.
- **[RMA: Rapid Motor Adaptation for Legged Robots](https://arxiv.org/abs/2107.04034)** (2021) — An adaptation module infers environment parameters online, so one policy handles new terrains and payloads in real time.
- **[Learning Quadrupedal Locomotion over Challenging Terrain](https://arxiv.org/abs/2010.11251)** (2020) — A teacher-student, proprioception-only policy that walks ANYmal over mud, snow and rubble in the wild.
- **[Learning Agile and Dynamic Motor Skills for Legged Robots](https://arxiv.org/abs/1901.08652)** (2019) — A learned actuator model closes the sim-to-real gap so RL policies run and recover from falls on ANYmal.

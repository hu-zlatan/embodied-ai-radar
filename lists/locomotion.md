- ⭐ **[BeyondMimic: From Motion Tracking to Versatile Humanoid Control via Guided Diffusion](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.08241)** (2025) — One motion-tracking recipe with shared hyperparameters learns agile skills (cartwheels, flip-kicks); a guided latent diffusion model composes them for new goals like joystick control, zero-shot on hardware. · [code](https://github.com/HybridRobotics/whole_body_tracking)
- ⭐ **[SONIC: Supersizing Motion Tracking for Natural Humanoid Whole-Body Control](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.07820)** (2025) — Tests scaling on humanoid control: more network, data and compute yield a generalist motion-tracking foundation model, with no manual reward engineering.
- ⭐ **[Real-World Humanoid Locomotion with Reinforcement Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.03381)** (2023) — A causal Transformer policy trained in simulation walks a full-size humanoid outdoors, zero-shot.
- ⭐ **[Learning to Walk in Minutes Using Massively Parallel Deep Reinforcement Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2109.11978)** (2021) — Thousands of parallel GPU environments train a quadruped walking policy in minutes; legged_gym became a standard starter. · [code](https://github.com/leggedrobotics/legged_gym)
- ⭐ **[DeepMimic: Example-Guided Deep Reinforcement Learning of Physics-Based Character Skills](https://arxiv.org/abs/1804.02717)** (2018) — Showed RL can imitate a wide range of motion clips, flips and spins included, while staying robust. The motion-imitation template much humanoid work builds on. · [code](https://github.com/xbpeng/DeepMimic)
- **[$λ_0$: Scaling Open-World Egocentric Human Data for Humanoid Loco-Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.18950)** (2026) — HumanVerse, 500 h of egocentric human video with body and hand motion, aligned to the Unitree G1 for mid-training a humanoid model; reports 90% on SIMPLE and 62.5% on real loco-manipulation tasks. · [code](https://github.com/LambdaHumanoid/openlambda) · [project](https://lambdahumanoid.github.io/)
- **[Action-Aligned Latent Planning for Versatile Humanoid Loco-Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.24675)** (2026) — FIBER plans in the latent space of a frozen behavioral foundation model: skills are projected into it, one perceptive flow planner is trained offline. Reports 42.0% mean sim success (latent replay: 11.6%); real G1. · [project](https://fiberaa.github.io/)
- **[CMP: Robust Whole-Body Tracking for Loco-Manipulation via Competence Manifold Projection](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.07457)** (2026) — CoRL 2026 Spotlight. Hardens whole-body end-effector tracking for legged manipulators against out-of-distribution inputs such as sensor noise or infeasible commands, using a frame-wise single-step safety manifold. · [project](https://shepherd1226.github.io/CMP)
- **[Coordinating Humanoid Loco-Manipulation through Action-Conditioned Latent Foresight](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.04839)** (2026) — A latent world-action model scores proposed humanoid command sequences before running them and replans each step; reports 85.7% on seven real tasks against 70.0% for GR00T N1.7. · [project](https://ju6276.github.io/Action-Grounded-Foresight/)
- **[DiffContact: Differentiable Contact Dynamics for Policy Learning and Contact-Implicit Trajectory Optimization](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.35852)** (2026) — Smooth analytic contact gradients only in the backward pass, hard-contact forward rollout unchanged: a quadruped trotting policy trains in under 2 min on a desktop and transfers to hardware without fine-tuning. · [project](https://diffcontact.github.io)
- **[DiffWBC: Unlocking Steerable General Humanoid Motion Tracking via One-Step Diffusion Whole-Body Control](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.41354)** (2026) — Humanoid tracker as a one-step diffusion model: one forward pass per 50 Hz step, test-time steering via cost gradients, failure discovery from plan dispersion without simulation. Deployed on a Unitree G1. · [project](https://diffwbc.github.io/)
- **[ELEVATE: Agentic Controller Acquisition from Task Failure for Quadrupedal Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.38388)** (2026) — When a quadruped manipulator keeps failing, a coding agent writes an RL problem to learn the missing whole-body skill, adds it to a library and retries; transfers to a real robot (18/20 rack retrieval). · [project](https://agentic-quad-elevate.github.io/public/)
- **[Energy-Based Policy Orchestration](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.02546)** (2026) — Picks at runtime which low-level policy to run and with which goal, using a learned energy per policy. New policies join by training their own energy model, with no retraining of others. Deployed on a real humanoid. · [project](https://yewr.github.io/epo)
- **[FORGE: Feasible Online Reduced-Order Reference Generation and Whole-Body Execution for Agile Humanoid Locomotion](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.37974)** (2026) — Learns a reduced-order reference generator, then trains a whole-body tracker on its online rollouts, so one pair of policies covers many gaits. On a Unitree G1: -2.0 to 2.8 m/s, hopping and continuous transitions.
- **[GRAFT: Geometry-Aware Residual Adaptation for Foundation Trackers](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.36507)** (2026) — Keeps a humanoid behavior foundation model frozen and trains a small lower-body residual adapter for stairs, gaps and platforms, distilled to onboard depth; tested on four trackers and on a Unitree G1 with SONIC. · [project](https://graft-humanoid.github.io/)
- **[GRAIL: Generating Humanoid Loco-Manipulation from World Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.05160)** (2026) — CoRL 2026 Oral. A fully virtual pipeline for humanoid loco-manipulation data: grounds video world models in 3D assets and simulator-ready scenes, with no teleoperation or physical setups. · [code](https://github.com/NVlabs/GRAIL) · [project](https://research.nvidia.com/labs/dair/grail/)
- **[HIPC: Humanoid Iterative Planning and Control via Goal-Conditioned Motion Closure](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.41334)** (2026) — Closes the loop between a generative motion model and a humanoid tracker, which usually run open-loop and drift. Deviation-decayed recovery and history re-anchoring keep the feedback from causing jitter or falls. · [project](https://hipc-humanoid.github.io/)
- **[HLO-AMP: Hierarchical Loco-Manipulation from Object-Aware Motion Priors](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.05113)** (2026) — Trains the low-level humanoid controller by motion imitation with the object physically in the scene, then a high-level policy on top; on a Unitree G1 it carries varied boxes and recovers from drops.
- **[HoloBoxing: A Simple Recipe for Humanoid Boxing with 7 Minutes of Single-GPU Training](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.32909)** (2026) — A compact planner sends body-point targets to a frozen pretrained whole-body controller; the seven minutes excludes controller pretraining. Evaluated in simulation.
- **[HuG: Learning Humanoid Whole-Body Affordance-Aware Grasping with Active Perception](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.01659)** (2026) — Trained in simulation with no real demos. Controller-in-the-loop filtering rejects 73% of kinematically valid grasps that would destabilize the robot; reports 87.5% real-world success on a multi-level shelf task. · [project](https://xuanbinpeng.com/hug)
- **[HuMiT: Low-Latency Whole-Body Humanoid Teleoperation via Compact Reference Tracking](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.29903)** (2026) — Whole-body humanoid teleoperation from a compact target (root height and velocity + 4 keypoint poses, current frame only): ~20 ms headset-to-policy latency; a Unitree G1 catches a tossed ball. · [code](https://github.com/xiaohu-art/Track) · [project](https://xiaohu-art.github.io/Track/)
- **[Humanoid-GPT: Scaling Data and Structure for Zero-Shot Motion Tracking](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.03985)** (2026) — A GPT-style causal Transformer pretrained on a 2B-frame retargeted motion corpus, tracking dynamic whole-body motion with zero-shot generalization to unseen motions. · [code](https://github.com/GalaxyGeneralRobotics/Humanoid-GPT) · [project](https://qizekun.github.io/Humanoid-GPT/)
- **[HumanoidNDM: Closing the Training-Deployment Gap of Motion Tracking via Humanoid Neural Dynamics Model](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.34126)** (2026) — Fine-tunes humanoid motion-tracking policies online on the real robot: a proprioceptive neural dynamics model learned from real data supplies gradients, no motion capture needed. Shown on Unitree G1.
- **[LadderMan: Learning Humanoid Perceptive Ladder Climbing](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.05873)** (2026) — CoRL 2026 Spotlight. A humanoid system for climbing diverse ladders and manipulating while on them; a two-stage pipeline learns climbing experts through hybrid motion tracking. · [project](https://ladderman-robot.github.io)
- **[Learning Force-Reactive Humanoid Loco-Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.35862)** (2026) — FLoRe: robot-free demos from force-sensing UMI-style grippers + body trackers train a diffusion policy that outputs per-hand force commands for a compliant whole-body controller; reports 80% vs. 26.7% for position-only. · [project](https://louis-leee.github.io/FLoRe/)
- **[MAPO: Mixture of Experts with Alternating Policy Optimization for Universal Humanoid Motion Tracking](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.02613)** (2026) — Dynamic routing makes policy-update divergence misestimated in mixture-of-experts policies, so MAPO optimizes router and experts in turn. Reports tracking 182 motions PPO with MoE fails on, against one the other way. · [project](https://mapo-corl2026.github.io/MAPO/)
- **[METATRON: A Promptable Whole-Body Execution Layer for Humanoids](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.38027)** (2026) — One frozen humanoid policy driven by prompts (timed body-part targets, velocity, style); skills are plain code, refined by coding agents. On a Unitree G1 it walks, recovers, picks up objects and sits. · [project](https://research.nvidia.com/labs/par/projects/metatron/)
- **[Movens-Zero: Scaling Humanoid Motion Foundation Models with Large-Scale Human Videos](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.41877)** (2026) — Movens-5M: 5M execution-verified humanoid motions from web videos, aligned with text, RGB, speech and music. Movens-Zero trains one flow-matching action expert on seven tasks for real-time closed-loop control. · [code](https://github.com/wendell0218/Movens-Zero) · [project](https://movens-zero.github.io)
- **[OpenHLM: An Empirical Recipe for Whole-Body Humanoid Loco-Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.22174)** (2026) — CoRL 2026 Spotlight. A one-variable-at-a-time empirical study of what a whole-body native VLA needs, mapping language and pixels directly to all humanoid degrees of freedom. · [code](https://github.com/OpenHLM-project/OpenHLM) · [project](https://openhlm-project.github.io/)
- **[REACH: An RL-based Hierarchical Humanoid Crawling Framework for Adaptable Confined Space Traversal](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.36546)** (2026) — Unitree G1 walks at variable height (1.27–0.50 m) and crawls (0.50–0.20 m), with a learned transition policy between modes and a LiDAR planner that sets height targets for confined-space traversal on hardware. · [project](https://aislab-sustech.github.io/REACH/)
- **[ROOF: Learning Humanoid Fall Recovery under Overhead Constraints](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.42097)** (2026) — Fall recovery beneath overhead obstacles from proprioception alone: rewards reducing robot-obstacle overlap, no prescribed path. Reports 95 of 150 recoveries on a Unitree G1 across seven real obstacle scenes. · [project](https://lu-yidan.github.io/ROOF/)
- **[RoboNaldo: Accurate, Stable and Powerful Humanoid Soccer Shooting via Motion-Guided Curriculum Reinforcement Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.11092)** (2026) — CoRL 2026 Oral. Three-stage curriculum for humanoid soccer shooting: a single human-kick reference serves as a scaffold, bridging stable motion tracking and goal-driven RL that struggles to discover kicks.
- **[SMR: Learning streaming motion retargeting for humanoids from human semantic](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.29169)** (2026) — SMR distils physics experts that track robot-aware targets into a strictly causal Transformer, retargeting human motion to humanoids frame by frame. Reports better closed-loop results than kinematic baselines. · [project](https://logosroboticsgroup.github.io/SMR/)
- **[SONIC-K1: Porting a Universal Humanoid Motion Tracker to a Small Humanoid on a Single GPU](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.39327)** (2026) — Open-weights whole-body motion tracker for the small Booster K1, adapting SONIC and trained on one shared GPU. Tracks most held-out clips in MuJoCo; on hardware, upper-body and slow motions work, stepping less so. · [code](https://github.com/Kyk0/sonic-k1) · [project](https://kyry.me/sonic-k1)

- **[SWAP: Test-Time Steerable Humanoid Whole-Body Collision Avoidance](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.25711)** (2026) — Diffusion planner proposes multimodal collision-aware motions; a whole-body RL controller tracks them. - **[Solving a Giant Rubik's Cube with a Quadruped Robot](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.24610)** (2026) — A quadruped solves a giant Rubik's Cube with its feet: onboard RGB-D estimates cube pose and colours, a planner sequences skills, and RL whole-body skills turn layers. - **[SuperMimic: Scaling Humanoid Whole-Body Control with One Differentiable World Model](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.28480)** (2026) — Rather than distilling per-motion experts or brute-forcing one RL policy, trains a humanoid tracking policy jointly with one differentiable world model of shared dynamics, fit from simulator logs (CoRL 2026 oral). · [project](https://supermimic.github.io)
- **[TACOS: Terrain-Aware COntact Supervision for Visual Footstep Planning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.37806)** (2026) — Scores footstep instability from factor-graph residuals in legged state estimation and projects it into earlier images to supervise a dense visual predictor. · [code](https://github.com/norlab-ulaval/TACOS)

- **[Watch Your Step: Anticipated Perception for Humanoid Locomotion](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.29612)** (2026) — A lightweight foothold prediction model strips terrain irrelevant to the next steps from the elevation map before the humanoid policy sees it, aiming at faster training and better transfer to new terrain. · [code](https://github.com/leggedrobotics/foothold-prediction-model) · [project](https://leggedrobotics.github.io/watch-your-step)
- **[Whole-Body Humanoid Control via Stochastic Motion Tokenization](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.36452)** (2026) — A stochastic motion tokenizer gives a high-level generator a compact interface to a whole-body tracking policy; reports better G1 motion reconstruction than FSQ/VQ-VAE and real dance/fight/walk sequences.
- **[Zero-HLM: Generalizable Humanoid Loco-Manipulation without Real Robot Data](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.35075)** (2026) — Real-to-sim-to-real: smartphone scans become physics-ready sim scenes, whole-body motions are corrected by rollouts, giving 4,800 demos; a sim-only GR00T N1.7 on Unitree G1 reports 66.7% vs. 46.7% trained on real data. · [code](https://github.com/BIGAI-General-Vision-Lab/Zero-HLM) · [project](https://young-bit.github.io/zero-hlm/)

- **[Unified Video Action Model](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.00200)** (2025) — UVA — one shared latent for video and actions with two light diffusion heads; masked training lets the same model act as policy, video generator and forward/inverse dynamics model, while keeping policy inference fast. · [code](https://github.com/ShuangLI59/unified_video_action)
- **[V-JEPA 2: Self-Supervised Video Models Enable Understanding, Prediction and Planning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.09985)** (2025) — A self-supervised video encoder, post-trained with a little robot data, plans zero-shot pick-and-place by latent prediction.
- **[Video Prediction Policy: A Generalist Robot Policy with Predictive Visual Representations](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.14803)** (2025) — VPP — fine-tunes Stable Video Diffusion on robot and human videos and feeds its one-pass predictive features to a diffusion policy; reports 4.33 average length on CALVIN ABC→D and real Franka / dexterous-hand results. · [code](https://github.com/roboterax/video-prediction-policy)
- **[World Simulation with Video Foundation Models for Physical AI](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.00062)** (2025) — A flow-based world foundation model unifying Text2World, Image2World and Video2World, released at 2B and 14B, with Cosmos-Transfer2.5 for Sim2Real translation. · [code](https://github.com/nvidia-cosmos/cosmos-predict2.5)
- **[WorldVLA: Towards Autoregressive Action World Model](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.21539)** (2025) — One autoregressive model that is both a VLA policy and an image-predicting world model; the two help each other on LIBERO, and an attention mask hiding earlier actions curbs error build-up in action chunks. · [code](https://github.com/alibaba-damo-academy/WorldVLA)
- **[DINO-WM: World Models on Pre-trained Visual Features enable Zero-shot Planning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.04983)** (2024) — Predicts future DINOv2 patch features instead of pixels, so a world model trained on offline data can plan to new visual goals at test time. · [code](https://github.com/gaoyuezhou/dino_wm)
- **[Genie: Generative Interactive Environments](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.15391)** (2024) — Learns playable environments and latent actions from unlabeled internet videos alone.
- **[Hierarchical World Models as Visual Whole-Body Humanoid Controllers](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.18418)** (2024) — Hierarchical world model for visual whole-body humanoid control: a high-level agent commands a low-level one, with no reward design or skill primitives. · [code](https://github.com/nicklashansen/puppeteer) · [project](https://nicklashansen.com/rlpuppeteer)
- **[Unleashing Large-Scale Video Generative Pre-training for Visual Robot Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.13139)** (2024) — GR-1 — a GPT-style model that predicts both actions and future frames; pre-training on Ego4D video prediction before robot fine-tuning lifts CALVIN unseen-scene success from 53.3% to 85.4% (as reported). · [code](https://github.com/bytedance/GR-1)
- **[Learning Interactive Real-World Simulators](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.06114)** (2023) — UniSim — an action-conditioned video model used as a simulator to train policies that transfer to the real world.
- **[Learning Universal Policies via Text-Guided Video Generation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2302.00111)** (2023) — UniPi — casts decision making as text-conditioned video generation, then recovers actions from the generated frames with an inverse dynamics model.
- **[TD-MPC2: Scalable, Robust World Models for Continuous Control](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.16828)** (2023) — An implicit (decoder-free) world model planned with MPC; one set of hyperparameters works across many continuous-control tasks and scales to a multitask agent. · [code](https://github.com/nicklashansen/tdmpc2)
- **[Transformers are Sample-Efficient World Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2209.00588)** (2023) — IRIS — a discrete autoencoder turns frames into tokens and a Transformer predicts the next tokens; the agent learns in imagination with only ~2 hours of Atari gameplay. · [code](https://github.com/eloialonso/iris)
- **[DayDreamer: World Models for Physical Robot Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2206.14176)** (2022) — Dreamer trained directly on real robots — a quadruped learns to walk in about an hour with no simulator.
- **[Temporal Difference Learning for Model Predictive Control](https://arxiv.org/abs/2203.04955)** (2022) — TD-MPC — a task-oriented latent model used for short-horizon planning, with a learned value function covering the long-term return; the predecessor of TD-MPC2. · [code](https://github.com/nicklashansen/tdmpc)
- **[Mastering Atari with Discrete World Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2010.02193)** (2021) — DreamerV2 — switches Dreamer to categorical latent variables and learns Atari behaviour purely inside the world model, reaching human-level scores. · [code](https://github.com/danijar/dreamerv2)
- **[Dream to Control: Learning Behaviors by Latent Imagination](https://arxiv.org/abs/1912.01603)** (2019) — The first Dreamer — learns a latent world model from pixels and trains the policy entirely on imagined rollouts.
- **[Learning Latent Dynamics for Planning from Pixels](https://arxiv.org/abs/1811.04551)** (2019) — PlaNet — learns a latent dynamics model (the RSSM later reused by Dreamer) from images and plans actions directly in latent space with CEM. · [code](https://github.com/google-research/planet)
- **[World Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/1803.10122)** (2018) — The classic VAE + RNN agent that learns a compressed model of its environment and trains a controller inside the "dream". · [project](https://worldmodels.github.io/)

- **[Envisioning Agentic Robotics: A New Era of Robotic Systems](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.18359)** — Position paper on agentic robotics: Embodied System 2, a frontier model plus an embodied harness, as the decision core; proposes an evaluation framework and a roadmap to sustained autonomy. / 关于智能体式机器人（agentic robotics）的路线图文章：提出由前沿模型加具身框架构成的“具身系统 2”作为决策核心，给出兼顾模型能力与闭环表现的评测框架，并规划从执行单个原语到持续自主工作的路径。 · [details · 详情](https://embodied-ai-radar.com/e/arxiv-2611.18359.html)
- **[ForceHarness: Turning Force into Action and Physics for Frozen Frontier Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.21099)** — Force feedback for frozen models: self-terminating primitives close the loop beneath the model; a physics layer returns quantities with uncertainty. Reports GPT-6 Astra on a real arm rising 3.3% to 90.0%. / 让只能调用 API 的冻结前沿模型获得力反馈：自终止力原语在模型之下闭环控制力，物理翻译层把力迹转为带不确定性的物理量。报告无腕部力传感器的真机机械臂上 GPT-6 Astra 零样本成功率从 3.3% 升到 90.0%。 · [details · 详情](https://embodied-ai-radar.com/e/arxiv-2611.21099.html)
- **[ScaleVLA: Scaling Vision-Language-Action Models to 552 Billion Parameters](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.21607)** — Connects a 552B DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash backbone to a trainable action DiT. Reports 10 demos beating a 2B backbone trained on 50 (87.8% vs 85.6%), LIBERO-PRO 45.1% vs at most 31.3%, and real charger insertion 9/10 vs 4/10. / 把 552B 的 DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash 主干接到可训练的动作 DiT，并在统一设置下对比 2B–552B 主干。报告 10 条示教胜过 2B 主干训练 50 条（87.8% 对 85.6%），LIBERO-PRO 45.1%（小模型最高 31.3%），真机充电器插入 9/10 对 4/10。 · [details · 详情](https://embodied-ai-radar.com/e/arxiv-2611.21607.html)
- **[TCP4D: A Unified Tool-Center-Point Tracking and Generation Model for Robot Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.23143)** — One 4D scene representation both tracks TCP motion and generates future TCP trajectories, retargeting motion into demos for new embodiments. Reports 7.60/16.07 mm ATE and 69.19% RoboTwin success. / 用共享 4D 场景表征同时完成工具中心点（TCP）运动跟踪与未来轨迹生成，并把恢复的运动重定向为新本体的演示数据。报告 DROID/RoboLab 轨迹误差 7.60/16.07 mm，RoboTwin 平均成功率 69.19%；源数据与增强数据联合训练再提高 41.5%。 · [details · 详情](https://embodied-ai-radar.com/e/arxiv-2611.23143.html)
- **[Heave-Ho: Decentralized Long-Horizon Multi-Humanoid Collaboration](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.22275)** — Post-trains a humanoid foundation tracking model for physically coupled multi-robot interaction; a vision-language planner coordinates decentralized execution. Trained on two, scales to 2-10 humanoids. / 去中心化的多个人形协作：多智能体后训练把基础跟踪模型适配到物理耦合交互，视觉语言规划器配合基于语音的领航-跟随同步实现无网络通信的协同执行。用两台人形训练，可扩展到 2–10 台，含 12 任务的 HH-Bench 与真机演示。 · [details · 详情](https://embodied-ai-radar.com/e/arxiv-2611.22275.html)
- **[HINT-WAM: Hindsight-Informed Training for World Action Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.24789)** — On-policy self-distillation for WAMs: the student acts on its own denoising trajectory while the same model conditioned on hindsight video provides dense targets, distilled via an energy score. / 首个 WAM 的在线自蒸馏框架：学生沿自身去噪轨迹生成动作，同一模型额外以“事后视频”（演示的未来帧）为条件充当教师，给出稠密的条件动作分布作为目标，再用能量分数在分布层面蒸馏——不依赖环境奖励或仿真交互，学生只看到部署期输入。 · [details · 详情](https://embodied-ai-radar.com/e/arxiv-2611.24789.html)
- **[WAM-Policy: World Action Models with On-Policy Distillation for Quadrupedal Loco-Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.24011)** — Turns an arm-centric WAM into a quadrupedal loco-manipulation policy: adaptation, self-distillation to one video + two steps, RL and multi-teacher merging. Reports 85.0% on QLM-Bench. / 把面向机械臂的 WAM 变成语言条件的四足移动操作策略：跨形态适配后，用在线自蒸馏把策略压到“一段视频+两步动作”的学生（采样快 14 倍），再以强化学习精调专家并多教师合并。报告 QLM-Bench 十个任务 85.0%，并在真实 Unitree Go2 Air 上部署。 · [details · 详情](https://embodied-ai-radar.com/e/arxiv-2611.24011.html)
- **[SALA: Semantically Aligned Latent Actions Enable Dual-Axis Scaling of Embodied Foundation Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.17110)** — Semantically aligns human and robot latent actions using language descriptions of unpaired clips; robot action reconstruction grounds control. Reports more pretraining video lowering robot action error. / 用无配对人类/机器人视频的语言描述估计语义相似度，监督跨本体潜动作的软邻域对齐，同时以机器人动作重建为控制提供根基。报告预训练人类视频越多、下游动作误差越低，任务专用视频越多、真机成功率越高。 · [details · 详情](https://embodied-ai-radar.com/e/arxiv-2611.17110.html)

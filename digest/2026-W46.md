## Editor's picks · 本周精选

_Top 8 of 92 new picks, ranked by community signals (Hugging Face upvotes, GitHub stars). 本周新增 92 条精选，按社区信号（Hugging Face 点赞、GitHub 星标）列出前 8 条。_

- **[Learning Heterogeneous Tactile Representation for General Dexterous Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.12806)** — LingBot-Touch learns one representation across piezoresistive, magnetic and visuotactile sensors. Its tactile tokens plug into diffusion policies and VLAs (π0.5, LingBot-VLA); gains in sim and on real robots. / LingBot-Touch 为压阻式、磁式和视触觉等不同触觉传感器学习统一表征，且易于扩展到新传感器。其触觉 token 可即插即用地接入扩散策略和 VLA（π0.5、LingBot-VLA），报告在仿真和多种真实平台上提升灵巧操作。 · [details · 详情](https://embodied-ai-radar.com/e/arxiv-2611.12806.html)
- **[World In-Context Learning for Agentic Robot Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.12885)** — RoboScript: a frozen model rehearses a task in sim from one demo and writes feedback into a task memory, not weights. 83.4% on all 50 RoboTwin 2.0 tasks, 20+ points over the best trained policy; real dual-arm too. / RoboScript：冻结的基础模型从一条示范出发在仿真中“排练”任务，把反馈写进任务记忆而不是权重。报告在 RoboTwin 2.0 全部 50 个任务上平均成功率 83.4%，比最强训练策略高 20 多个点，并迁移到真实双臂机器人。 · [details · 详情](https://embodied-ai-radar.com/e/arxiv-2611.12885.html)
- **[Toward the Era of Agentic Embodied Intelligence](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.09124)** — A²-MEAS pairs a VLA with motion history and a latent world model (A²-WLA) with agents that write learned 'correction codes'. Reports 0.641 Q on all 100 BEHAVIOR 2026 public tasks and 94% on RoboTwin 2.0. / A²-MEAS 把带运动历史和潜在世界模型的 VLA（A²-WLA）与生成“纠错代码”的多智能体结合，训练时吸收进权重，部署时冻结权重也能纠错。报告 BEHAVIOR 2026 全部 100 个公开任务 Q 分 0.641，RoboTwin 2.0 约 94%。 · [details · 详情](https://embodied-ai-radar.com/e/arxiv-2611.09124.html)
- **[On Agentic Manipulation Policies](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.09892)** — A generalist AI model commands the arm directly through a reference harness (AMP1), with no demos or built-in grasp skills; reports 85.6% zero-shot on RoboLab-120 vs 49.2% for the best VLA, and 104 of 108 real trials. / 让通用 AI 模型通过参考框架 AMP1 直接指挥机械臂，无需示教，也不预置抓取技能；在 RoboLab-120 上零样本成功率 85.6%（最佳 VLA 为 49.2%），真机 108 次试验成功 104 次。 · [details · 详情](https://embodied-ai-radar.com/e/arxiv-2611.09892.html)
- **[Embodied First Exam](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.12177)** — Benchmark for coding agents that control robots their own way (direct, code, or training a policy): 1,156 tasks, 35 suites, 47 robots. The best of eight agents solves 34% of the 456 core tasks in the realistic setting. / 面向“用编码智能体控制机器人”的基准：智能体可自选直接控制、写代码或训练策略，共 1,156 个任务、35 个套件、47 种机器人。在贴近真实的标准设定下，八个智能体中最强者在 456 个核心任务上成功率仅 34%。 · [details · 详情](https://embodied-ai-radar.com/e/arxiv-2611.12177.html)
- **[DexForesight: Grounding Dexterous Policies with Implicit Spatial Foresight](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.11166)** — Trains a world model of 3D hand-point and scene motion, then aligns policy latents to it; at test time only the RGB policy runs, with no depth or future frames. Reports 49.4% → 60.5% on 11 DexJoCo tasks. / 先训练预测 3D 手部点与场景变化的世界模型，再把策略潜表示对齐到它；部署时只保留 RGB 策略，无需深度或未来预测。在 11 个 DexJoCo 任务上成功率从 49.4% 提升到 60.5%。 · [details · 详情](https://embodied-ai-radar.com/e/arxiv-2611.11166.html)
- **[SWAM: Scaling World Action Models with Cross-Modal Alignment](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.12870)** — How to keep language-video alignment while a world action model learns actions, studied on ~47k hours of general, egocentric, UMI and robot video. Egocentric + UMI data helps; data, code and weights released. / 在约 4.7 万小时的通用视频、第一人称视频、UMI 与机器人示范上，系统研究世界动作模型学习动作时如何保持语言-视频对齐。混合第一人称视频与 UMI 数据提升控制泛化；开放数据、代码与权重。 · [details · 详情](https://embodied-ai-radar.com/e/arxiv-2611.12870.html)
- **[Learning the Task or Learning the Verifier? Rethinking Criteria Exposure in Embodied Self-Improvement](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.08318)** — Self-improving coding agents that see a benchmark's success checks score higher, but the gain vanishes when checks are slightly reworded and skills transfer poorly; proposes instruction-faithful criteria. / 让自我改进的编程智能体看到基准的成功判定条件后，分数很快上升，但判定条件稍作改写（不偏离指令）优势就消失，学到的技能也难跨任务迁移；作者提出更忠实于指令的判定标准。 · [details · 详情](https://embodied-ai-radar.com/e/arxiv-2611.08318.html)

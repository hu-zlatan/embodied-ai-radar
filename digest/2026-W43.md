_Top 8 of 63 new picks, ranked by community signals (Hugging Face upvotes, GitHub stars). 本周新增 63 条精选，按社区信号（Hugging Face 点赞、GitHub 星标）列出前 8 条。_

- **[Has GPT-6-Astra Achieved Embodied Intelligence?](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.22471)** — Drives robots with a frozen frontier LLM plus in-context 'knowledge packages' on 12 sim benchmarks + 8 real robots; reports beating prior bests on 9 of 12, but weak real-robot results (competence, not yet intelligence). / 用冻结的前沿大模型加上下文“知识包”直接控制机器人，覆盖 12 个仿真基准和 8 台真机；报告在 12 个基准中的 9 个超过此前最好结果，但真机表现较弱——结论是具备了具身“能力”，尚未达到具身“智能”。 · [details · 详情](https://hu-zlatan.github.io/embodied-ai-radar/e/arxiv-2610.22471.html)
- **[DONIC: Generalist Dexterous Bimanual Tracking Control from Human Demonstrations](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.29997)** — One two-hand policy (PPO + BC on its own verified successes) tracks retargeted human hand-object motions; ships BiHOI (1,575 sequences, 229 objects). Reports 86.8% on unseen trajectories, 57.3% on new objects. / 用一个双手跟踪策略（PPO 结合对自身成功执行的行为克隆）复现重定向后的人手-物体交互动作，并发布 BiHOI 数据集（1575 段双手序列、229 个物体）。论文报告在未见轨迹上成功率 86.8%、新物体上 57.3%。 · [details · 详情](https://hu-zlatan.github.io/embodied-ai-radar/e/arxiv-2610.29997.html)
- **[Ropedia W0: Scaling Loco-Manipulation World Models across Embodiments with Human Experience](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.26855)** — An egocentric world model covering both walking and manipulating, trained on ~10k hours of human video plus ~3k hours of robot data under one shared kinematic action representation. / 同时覆盖移动与操作的第一人称世界模型，在约 1 万小时人类视频和约 3 千小时机器人数据上训练，用统一的运动学表示描述不同本体的动作。 · [details · 详情](https://hu-zlatan.github.io/embodied-ai-radar/e/arxiv-2610.26855.html)
- **[Learning from Fleet Experience: Collective Experience Learning Across 96 Robots](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.25736)** — Deployed robots pool their runs; failures retrieve matching successful segments from the fleet buffer to fine-tune on, with no new demos. Reports 93–99% on five real tasks; 96 robots lift phone packing 79% → 98%. / 部署中的机器人共享执行经验：用失败片段在机群经验库中检索相似的成功片段来微调策略，无需新增示教或人工纠正。论文报告五个真机任务成功率 93–99%，96 台机器人把手机装盒任务从 79% 提升到 98%。 · [details · 详情](https://hu-zlatan.github.io/embodied-ai-radar/e/arxiv-2610.25736.html)
- **[HexaShell: A Thin Harness for Exploring the Limits of VLM Agents as Robot Coding Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.23479)** — A coding agent drives a bimanual robot through one command line; after each failure it reads the logs and writes or fixes small reusable tool programs. / 编程智能体通过一条命令行驱动双臂机器人；每次失败后读取日志，编写或修复可复用的小工具程序。 · [details · 详情](https://hu-zlatan.github.io/embodied-ai-radar/e/arxiv-2610.23479.html)
- **[GreenWAM: A World Foundation Model for Robotics](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.18774)** — One world model that is also the policy, across 20 robot families mapped to a shared joint-space action format; reports strong WorldArena, RoboTwin 2.0 and LIBERO-plus results and a one-step distilled humanoid policy. / 同一个世界模型兼作策略，用共享的关节坐标动作空间覆盖 20 类机器人；论文报告其在 WorldArena、RoboTwin 2.0、LIBERO-plus 上表现突出，并蒸馏出可实时运行的单步人形机器人策略。 · [details · 详情](https://hu-zlatan.github.io/embodied-ai-radar/e/arxiv-2610.18774.html)
- **[ScaleNav: Scalable Internet Video Pretraining for Language-Conditioned Local Navigation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.25690)** — Pretrains on internet egocentric video to learn how the view changes when approaching a described target, then aligns to actions in sim; reports 94.9% success on its HabitatGS-LN benchmark plus a real quadruped. / 先在互联网第一视角视频上预训练，学习"朝描述的目标走近时画面如何变化"，再用仿真数据对齐到动作；论文报告在自建 HabitatGS-LN 基准上成功率 94.9%，并在真实四足机器人上部署。 · [details · 详情](https://hu-zlatan.github.io/embodied-ai-radar/e/arxiv-2610.25690.html)
- **[Scaling in Games: Continued Pre-Training For Embodied Agents In Diverse Virtual Worlds](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.28698)** — Continued pre-training study for game-playing agents: 120 runs over 0.8B–27B models, 50–1,600 h of data and multi-world mixes; reports diversity aids transfer to related worlds, little to distant ones. / 针对游戏中的具身智能体做持续预训练的对照研究：120 组配置覆盖 0.8B–27B 模型、50–1600 小时数据和多世界混合；报告数据多样性有助于迁移到相近世界，对差异大的世界帮助有限。 · [details · 详情](https://hu-zlatan.github.io/embodied-ai-radar/e/arxiv-2610.28698.html)

## Editor's picks · 本周精选

_Top 8 of 116 new picks, ranked by community signals (Hugging Face upvotes, GitHub stars). 本周新增 116 条精选，按社区信号（Hugging Face 点赞、GitHub 星标）列出前 8 条。_

- **[PDRVLA: A Vision-Language-Action Model with Physical Dynamics Representations](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.37381)** — Light module turns VLM features into 'physical tokens' trained to predict future RGB, depth and flow features (with world-model supervision); no online video generation. Reports 99.3% LIBERO, 93.6% RoboTwin 2.0. / 轻量物理动力学模块从视觉-语言特征中提取“物理 token”，通过预测未来 RGB、深度和光流特征（并辅以冻结视频世界模型监督）来学习，推理时无需在线生成视频；仅用公开数据训练，报告 LIBERO 99.3%、RoboTwin 2.0 93.6%。 · [details · 详情](https://hu-zlatan.github.io/embodied-ai-radar/e/arxiv-2610.37381.html)
- **[Rollback: Experience Backtracking in World Models for Policy Improvement](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.36068)** — Rewinds an action-conditioned world model to key decision points to try alternative actions, turning outcomes into action-level supervision; real data keeps correcting the model. Reports +10.7–11.9 pts on 3 real tasks. / 把动作条件世界模型当作反事实引擎：回到关键决策点尝试其他动作并对比结果，得到动作级监督而无需单独的价值模型；真机经验再通过 LoRA 持续修正世界模型。报告在 3 个真实任务上比最强基线高 10.7–11.9 个百分点。 · [details · 详情](https://hu-zlatan.github.io/embodied-ai-radar/e/arxiv-2610.36068.html)
- **[ThinkWorld: World Imagination Reinforces Chain-of-Dynamics Reasoning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.37666)** — VLA that reasons in discrete motion tokens (a chain of dynamics) shared across embodiments; predicted futures are scored against what actually happened, so action-free human videos give RL signal. Sim and real tests. / VLA 用跨本体共享的离散运动 token 链（Chain-of-Dynamics）表达动作思考；预测每条链导致的未来并与真实结果比对来强化，使没有动作标注的人类视频也能提供监督。在仿真与真机上验证人到机器人的迁移。 · [details · 详情](https://hu-zlatan.github.io/embodied-ai-radar/e/arxiv-2610.37666.html)
- **[RynnUnify: Towards Unified Embodied Brain](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.32965)** — A 16B model interleaving video prediction and language reasoning for embodied understanding and long-horizon visual planning, plus a world-action extension for control; reports 51.5% on 20 RoboCasa365 tasks. / 16B 统一具身模型，交替进行视频预测与语言推理，兼顾具身理解与长程视觉规划，并扩展出用于机器人控制的世界-动作版本；另提出 Rynn-VP、Rynn-GEU 评测，报告在 20 个 RoboCasa365 组合任务上成功率 51.5%。 · [details · 详情](https://hu-zlatan.github.io/embodied-ai-radar/e/arxiv-2610.32965.html)
- **[Sensorimotor Alignment: A Platonic Proxy for Embodied Policy Success](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31670)** — S² is a training-free offline metric of how well a model's representation aligns with physical motion. Reports it tracks robot task success across model families and can rank VLM backbones before VLA fine-tuning. / S² 是一种无需训练的离线指标，衡量模型视觉表征与物理运动几何的对齐程度（把柏拉图表征假说扩展到机器人）；报告它与多类模型的真实任务成功率正相关，可在微调成 VLA 之前筛选 VLM 骨干。 · [details · 详情](https://hu-zlatan.github.io/embodied-ai-radar/e/arxiv-2610.31670.html)
- **[HuGe: Scaling Humanoid Generalist Vision-Language-Action Models with Egocentric Experience](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.34186)** — Humanoid VLA family pre-trained on 2,000+ h of egocentric human and robot data, predicting latent motion tokens for a whole-body controller; uses pseudo-proprioception and arm re-rendering to bridge the embodiment gap. / 人形机器人通用 VLA 系列：在 2,000 多小时第一视角人类与机器人数据上预训练，输出潜在动作 token 驱动预训练的全身控制器并附手部信号；通过仿真回放构造伪本体感知、重渲染手臂外观缩小人-机差异，报告 HumanoidArena 最优，并在 Unitree G1 上验证人类数据增益。 · [details · 详情](https://hu-zlatan.github.io/embodied-ai-radar/e/arxiv-2610.34186.html)
- **[DiscoDemo: Discovering Efficient and Diverse Robot Demonstrations for Imitation Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.32093)** — Uses RL as a demo generator, not a controller: a latent-conditioned policy finds several distinct successful solutions, exported as an imitation dataset; reports 78% real-robot success vs. 39% for the best baseline. / 把强化学习当作示教数据生成器而非部署控制器：以隐变量为条件的策略为同一任务找到多种不同的成功解法，导出为模仿学习数据集；报告真机下游成功率从最强基线的 39% 提升到 78%。 · [details · 详情](https://hu-zlatan.github.io/embodied-ai-radar/e/arxiv-2610.32093.html)
- **[RynnVLA-Latent: Scaling Robotic Manipulation Foundation Models with 3D-Grounded Latent Actions](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.34356)** — Pretrains a 4B VLA on 3D-grounded latent actions mined from 30K hours of human, robot and sim video, then adapts to real robot actions. Reports 98.8% on LIBERO and 88.6% on RoboTwin 2.0. / 从 3 万小时人类、机器人与仿真视频中学习带 3D 约束的连续潜在动作（RynnLAM），用于预训练 4B 参数 VLA，再适配到具体机器人动作空间。报告 LIBERO 98.8%、RoboTwin 2.0 平均 88.6%。 · [details · 详情](https://hu-zlatan.github.io/embodied-ai-radar/e/arxiv-2610.34356.html)

> **This week in one paragraph.** Robot data without robots was the loudest thread: AIRoA-0 reports learning from 16,151 hours of handheld-gripper demos with no robot data, Open-HumanWAM opens a 50,000-hour egocentric corpus and reports a log-linear link between pretraining hours and success, and HALO trains humanoid skills on data synthesised in procedural homes. The second thread was a warning about scores: RoboColosseum's blinded trials at 124 sites find that rankings barely agree across robots, and SimuGuard traces many simulated "policy failures" to physics-engine artifacts. A third, smaller one: open harnesses that turn general models into robot agents (uni-robot-agent, CaPtain, PyRUA). CoRL 2026 opens on Nov 9; the [program guide](https://hu-zlatan.github.io/embodied-ai-radar/venues/corl-2026-program.html) lists all 687 accepted papers by session and poster, 555 with a short takeaway, and lets you save a personal schedule.

## Editor's picks · 本周精选

_Top 8 of 218 new picks, ranked by community signals (Hugging Face upvotes, GitHub stars). 本周新增 218 条精选，按社区信号（Hugging Face 点赞、GitHub 星标）列出前 8 条。_

- **[From Pixels to Actions - Towards Native Vision-Language-Action Policies](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.06918)** — NEO-VLA drops the visual encoder: images and language evolve in one backbone and a flow-matching action expert reads several depths. Layer probing shows action signals often peak before the final visual layer. / NEO-VLA 去掉独立视觉编码器：图像与语言在同一主干中共同演化，流匹配动作专家从多个深度读取特征。逐层探测显示，对动作关键的信号往往在最终视觉层之前达到峰值。 · [details · 详情](https://hu-zlatan.github.io/embodied-ai-radar/e/arxiv-2611.06918.html)
- **[RoboColosseum: Evaluating Physical Intelligence at Scale In-The-Wild](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.02497)** — Blinded pairwise trials at 124 sites compare VLAs and frontier agents. Rankings barely agree across robots (W = 0.24), and open rankings poorly predict results after fine-tuning; no policy finishes a G1 task. / 在 124 个站点做盲测两两对比，横跨四种机器人评测 VLA 与前沿智能体。不同机器人的排名几乎不一致（Kendall W = 0.24），开箱排名也难以预测微调后的表现；G1 人形上没有策略完成任何任务。 · [details · 详情](https://hu-zlatan.github.io/embodied-ai-radar/e/arxiv-2611.02497.html)
- **[Open-HumanWAM: An Open Recipe for Scaling Human and Robot Experience for Embodied Foundation Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.06817)** — Opens a 50,000-hour annotated egocentric video corpus with pipelines and checkpoints. Reports a log-linear law between pretraining hours and robot success, and 84.6% on RoboTwin 2.0 Clean2Random. / 开源 5 万小时带标注的第一视角视频语料及流程和权重。报告预训练时长与机器人成功率之间的对数线性规律，后训练后在 RoboTwin 2.0 Clean2Random 上达到 84.6%。 · [details · 详情](https://hu-zlatan.github.io/embodied-ai-radar/e/arxiv-2611.06817.html)
- **[Perceptive Humanoid Motion from Simulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.06908)** — HALO trains humanoid skills on vision-language-motion data synthesised in a thousand procedural homes, so data scales with compute. Reports zero-shot transfer: 91% over 360 trials in six real environments. / HALO 在上千个程序化生成的家庭场景中合成视觉-语言-动作数据来训练人形技能，数据量随算力增长。报告零微调迁移：六个未建图真实环境的 360 次试验成功率 91%。 · [details · 详情](https://hu-zlatan.github.io/embodied-ai-radar/e/arxiv-2611.06908.html)
- **[RoboJEPA: Scaling Robotic Latent World Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.10515)** — A JEPA world model trained across 12 robot embodiments; its imagination error follows a power law in compute, so model quality can be predicted beyond the fitted scale. / 跨 12 种机器人形态训练的 JEPA 世界模型；其想象误差随算力服从幂律，因此可以预测超出拟合规模时的模型质量。 · [details · 详情](https://hu-zlatan.github.io/embodied-ai-radar/e/arxiv-2610.10515.html)
- **[When the Simulator Fails, Not the Policy: Auditing Contact Artifacts in Simulated Robot Benchmarks](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.41857)** — Many benchmark 'policy failures' are physics-engine ejections. SimuGuard traced RoboTwin artifacts to one collision constant; changing it cut artifact episodes from 12/100 to none and lifted five tasks 89% → 98%. / 审计仿真基准：不少“策略失败”其实是物理引擎把物体弹飞。SimuGuard 为 LIBERO、RoboTwin、RoboCasa、RoboDojo 提供审计插件，把 RoboTwin 上的问题追溯到碰撞检测的一个常量；仅改动它，异常回合由 100 回合 12 次降到 0，五个容器任务成功率由 89% 升到 98%。 · [details · 详情](https://hu-zlatan.github.io/embodied-ai-radar/e/arxiv-2610.41857.html)
- **[AIRoA-0: Long-Horizon, Precise, Cross-Embodiment Manipulation from Handheld Data](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.03815)** — Learned from 16,151 hours of handheld gripper demos with no robot data; reports 78% average success over ten tasks versus 27% for the best other VLA, and releases the dataset, weights and post-training recipe. / 从 16,151 小时手持夹爪演示中学习，全程不采机器人数据；报告十项任务平均成功率 78%，其他 VLA 最好为 27%，并开放数据集、权重和后训练方案。 · [details · 详情](https://hu-zlatan.github.io/embodied-ai-radar/e/arxiv-2611.03815.html)
- **[Pixel: A Co-Designed Full-Stack Robot System for Learning General-Purpose Mobile Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.03648)** — Hardware, data and learning designed together: a low-cost wheeled humanoid with compliant grippers, teleoperation and robot-free human data collection, one whole-body controller and a shared policy pipeline. / 把硬件、数据和学习放在一起设计：低成本轮式人形、柔顺夹爪，遥操作与免机器人的人类数据采集，统一的全身控制器，以及人类与机器人示范共用的策略学习流程。 · [details · 详情](https://hu-zlatan.github.io/embodied-ai-radar/e/arxiv-2611.03648.html)
